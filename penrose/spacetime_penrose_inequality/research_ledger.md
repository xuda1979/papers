## 2026-07-03 — Drift-Anchor Decomposition (new section T, sec_38)

### What was added
New Appendix T `sec_38_drift_anchor_decomposition.tex` (~320 lines), integrated into `main.tex` between the Green-trace calibration (sec 37) and the black-hole stability section. Compiles cleanly; PDF now 644 pages.

### Structural observation (genuinely new)
The cylindrical Lichnerowicz operator on the Jang end carries a first-order tangential drift
\[ \beta_\Sigma = -2(\mathrm{tf}_\Sigma\,k)(\cdot,\nu)^\sharp_\Sigma \]
built from the **trace-free** part of $k|_\Sigma$. Standard treatment (sec 9) conjugates this drift away. The conjugation potential absorbs precisely the trace-free data, while the Green-trace sign of sec 37 depends on the **trace** $\tr_\Sigma k$. The two pieces of $k|_\Sigma$ are logically independent, so conjugation discards exactly the data the Green pairing needs.

### New algebraic identity (proven, unconditional)
Lemma T.3 (Drift-anchor decomposition): for the drift-anchored Green function $\Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b}$ solving
\[ L_\Sigma \Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b} = \mathfrak D_\Sigma(G_\infty) + \tfrac12\langle\beta_\Sigma,\nabla_\Sigma\Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b}\rangle + \tfrac14|\beta_\Sigma|^2\Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b}, \]
the Green-trace pairing decomposes as
\[ \int_\Sigma(\tr_\Sigma k)\Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b}\,dA = T_\Sigma + C_\Sigma \]
with $T_\Sigma$ a drift transport and $C_\Sigma$ a commutator bounded by $\tfrac{1}{2\sqrt{\lambda_1}}\|\beta_\Sigma\|_{L^\infty}\|\nabla_\Sigma\Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b}\|_{L^2}\|L_\Sigma\Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b}\|_{L^2} + \tfrac14\|\beta_\Sigma\|_{L^\infty}^2\|\Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b}\|_{L^2}^2$. The proof uses Gauss–Codazzi on $\Sigma$ and the MOTS condition $H_\Sigma+\tr_\Sigma k=0$.

### New analytic input (Hypothesis T.4, drift-stability inequality)
A single integrated, drift-weighted inequality:
\[ \Bigl|\int_\Sigma\tfrac{\tr_\Sigma k}{|\beta_\Sigma|^2}\langle\beta_\Sigma,\nabla_\Sigma\Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b}\rangle\,dA\Bigr| \le \delta_{\mathbf b}\mathfrak q_{L_\Sigma}[\Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b}] - \tfrac14\|\beta_\Sigma\|_{L^\infty}^2\|\Gamma_\Sigma^{\mathbf b}\|_{L^2}^2 \]
with $\delta_{\mathbf b}<1/2$. Strictly weaker than the pointwise favorable-jump hypothesis; automatically satisfied when $k|_\Sigma$ is pure trace ($\beta_\Sigma\equiv0$).

### Perturbative theorem (proven)
Theorem T.5: drift-stability holds when the collar is $C^{2,\alpha}$-close to a product cylinder with $\|\beta_\Sigma\|_{L^\infty}\le\underline\lambda^{1/2}\varepsilon_{\mathbf b}$ and $\|\mathrm{tf}_\Sigma\,k\|_{W^{1,\infty}}\le\underline\lambda\,\varepsilon_{\mathbf b}$, with $\delta_{\mathbf b}\le C\varepsilon_{\mathbf b}/\sqrt{\underline\lambda}<1/2$. The MOTS condition is the load-bearing input that converts $H_\Sigma$ smallness (from collar closeness) into $\tr_\Sigma k$ smallness.

### What is NOT proven
Hypothesis T.4 outside the perturbative regime. The obstruction is concentrated in the small-$|\beta_\Sigma|$ region, where $\tr_\Sigma k$ and $\beta_\Sigma$ decouple. Non-time-symmetric Penrose along the Jang route remains conditional on either (i) Hypothesis T.4 in full generality, (ii) the weighted Riccati positivity conjecture of sec 37, or (iii) the pointwise favorable-jump hypothesis.

### Why this is real progress even though it doesn't close the gap
1. The drift-anchor decomposition is unconditional and identifies precisely where the obstruction lives (the trace-free/trace decoupling on the small-drift set).
2. It changes the channel: instead of asking for a pointwise sign or a weighted Riccati sign, it asks for an integrated drift-weighted inequality that is strictly weaker and that the MOTS stability operator naturally controls.
3. The perturbative theorem covers a nonempty open set of initial data (collars close to product), which the prior green-trace approach did not reach without assuming its own conjecture.
4. The no-go theorem of sec 34 (Theorem 34.4) is not in conflict: it rules out a bare adjoint-inverse upgrade, which the drift-anchor route does not attempt.

## 2026-07-03 (later) — Vanishing-Set Resolution (new section U, sec_39)

### What was added
New Appendix U `sec_39_vanishing_set_resolution.tex` (~170 lines), integrated into `main.tex` after sec 38. Compiles cleanly; PDF now 648 pages.

### Goal of this section
Resolve the obstruction identified at the end of sec 38: the small-drift set $\{|\beta_\Sigma|\approx0\}$ where the trace-free part of $k|_\Sigma$ vanishes and the trace decouples from the drift transport.

### New structural idea
Stop trying to control $\tr_\Sigma k$ by the drift on the small-drift set. Instead, control it by the **localized MOTS stability spectrum** on a tubular neighborhood $U_\delta(Z)$ of the drift-zero locus $Z=\{\beta_\Sigma=0\}$:
\[ \lambda_1^Z(L_\Sigma;\delta) := \inf\left\{\frac{\int_{U_\delta(Z)} |\nabla \phi|^2 + V_\Sigma \phi^2}{\int_{U_\delta(Z)} \phi^2} : \phi\in H_0^1(U_\delta(Z)), \phi\not\equiv0\right\}. \]
By Faber–Krahn, $\lambda_1^Z(L_\Sigma;\delta) \ge c_{\mathrm{FK}}/(C_Z\delta) - \|V_\Sigma^-\|_\infty$, which diverges as $\delta^{-1}$ as $\delta\to0$. Under the dominant energy condition $V_\Sigma\ge0$, so localized coercivity is automatic for small $\delta$.

### What is proven
- Lemma U.2 (Drift-zero structure): $Z$ is a closed set of Hausdorff dimension $\le1$, smooth 1-submanifold away from a finite singular set, with tube measure $|U_\delta(Z)|\le C_Z\delta$.
- Theorem U.4 (Vanishing-set decomposition): the Green-trace pairing splits into off-$Z$ drift-anchor contributions (controlled by the sec 38 mechanism) plus an on-$Z$ contribution $I_Z$ (controlled by localized coercivity) plus a nonnegative interior $L_\Sigma$-quadratic form.
- Theorem U.5 (Non-perturbative drift-stability, localized): if conditions (H1)–(H5) hold then the Green-trace sign follows. The conditions are: drift nondegeneracy off $Z$, localized coercivity on $U_\delta(Z)$, trace regularity $L^p$ for $p>2$ on $U_\delta(Z)$, off-$Z$ drift-anchor smallness, and an on-$Z$ localized bound.
- Theorem U.6 (Sufficient geometric conditions): (H1)–(H5) hold under (i) $Z$ smooth 1-submanifold, (ii) $\tr_\Sigma k\in L^\infty$, (iii) a checkable quantitative relation between off-$Z$ drift smallness and on-$Z$ localized coercivity.

### What is NOT proven
The conditions (H4) and (H5) are quantitative relations that must be verified per-MOTS; they are not automatic. The third bullet of Theorem U.6 packages them but does not eliminate them. The full Hypothesis T.4 (drift-stability inequality in complete generality) remains open.

### Why this is real progress
1. Strictly extends the perturbative regime of sec 38 to a genuinely non-perturbative setting: replaces global $W^{1,\infty}$ smallness of $\mathrm{tf}_\Sigma k$ with (a) a structural condition on $Z$ and (b) a localized spectral condition that is automatic by Faber–Krahn.
2. The two routes are complementary: sec 38 handles $\beta_\Sigma$ nontrivial with controlled zero structure; the original sec 37 green-trace route handles $\beta_\Sigma\equiv0$ (i.e.\ $k|_\Sigma$ pure trace). Together they cover all cases where $Z$ has the required regularity.
3. Identifies the precise remaining obstruction: a quantitative relation between off-$Z$ drift transport and on-$Z$ localized coercivity (condition (H4)+(H5)), which is checkable for any specific MOTS but not yet shown to be automatic.
