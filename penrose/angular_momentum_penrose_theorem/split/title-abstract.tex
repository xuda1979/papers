\begin{abstract}
We derive the Angular Momentum Penrose Inequality for asymptotically flat, axisymmetric initial data $(M^3,g,K)$ satisfying the dominant energy condition with vacuum in the exterior region, containing an outermost strictly stable marginally outer trapped surface $\Sigma$ of area $A$ and angular momentum $J$. Under hypotheses (H1)--(H5) alone, we establish
\[
M_{\mathrm{ADM}} \geq \sqrt{\frac{A}{16\pi}+\frac{4\pi J^2}{A}},
\]
a bound saturated by Kerr slices. The proof proceeds via a four-stage Jang--conformal--AMO method: an axisymmetric Jang equation with twist perturbation, an angular-momentum-modified Lichnerowicz equation, angular-momentum conservation via Stokes' theorem, and the Dain--Reiris sub-extremality bound. The central innovation is the AM-Hawking mass $m_{H,J}(t)=\sqrt{m_H^2(t)+4\pi J^2/A(t)}$, which regularizes the area divergence at infinity while incorporating angular momentum.

The initial derivation assumes two auxiliary inputs: monotonicity of $m_{H,J}$ along the $p$-harmonic foliation and a conformal ADM mass comparison. A subsequent analytical engine---comprising a twisted Bochner monotonicity identity, a conformal cohomological mass comparison, a Kerr--Cartan integral identity, and a Weyl--Simon Codazzi calibration---then replaces these inputs by proven geometric formulae, yielding a strengthened theorem under hypotheses (H1)--(H5) alone. The equality case reduces unconditionally to the vanishing of the Mars--Simon tensor; the final Kerr isometry remains conditional on an external rigidity input. A twisted Callias--Dirac spectral mass formula furnishes an independent route, and a formal holographic entanglement argument is outlined as a speculative complement. Extensions to the charged (Kerr--Newman) case are developed.
\end{abstract}
