\begin{abstract}
We derive a conditional version of the Angular Momentum Penrose Inequality: for asymptotically flat, axisymmetric initial data $(M^3, g, K)$ satisfying the dominant energy condition with vacuum in the exterior region, containing an outermost strictly stable marginally outer trapped surface (MOTS) $\Sigma$ of area $A$ and Komar angular momentum $J$, and satisfying both an auxiliary AM-Hawking monotonicity input for the rotating $p$-harmonic flow and the conformal mass comparison input for the AM-Lichnerowicz deformation,
\[
M_{\ADM} \geq \sqrt{\frac{A}{16\pi} + \frac{4\pi J^2}{A}},
\]
and we verify directly that Kerr slices saturate this bound. The equality case is reduced to the vanishing of the Kerr deviation tensor; the final Kerr uniqueness step is therefore conditional on an external rigidity input rather than proved here from first principles.

The proof introduces a four-stage Jang--conformal--AMO method: (1)~solve an axisymmetric Jang equation with twist as a lower-order perturbation; (2)~solve an angular-momentum-modified Lichnerowicz equation; (3)~establish angular momentum conservation via Stokes' theorem for closed 2-forms; (4)~apply the Dain--Reiris sub-extremality bound. The key innovation is the AM-Hawking mass $m_{H,J}(t) := \sqrt{m_H^2(t) + 4\pi J^2/A(t)}$; the manuscript shows how an external monotonicity statement for this functional, together with the endpoint analysis carried out here, yields the inequality.

As a methodological application, we outline a new proof strategy for the Charged Penrose Inequality $M_{\ADM} \geq M_{\mathrm{irr}} + Q^2/(4M_{\mathrm{irr}})$ for non-rotating Einstein--Maxwell data, demonstrating the versatility of the Jang--conformal--AMO framework. This charged case is presented as a proof outline; complete rigorous proofs exist in the literature \cite{mars2009, khuri2015charged}.

\medskip

\textbf{Strengthened result.} We then develop a novel analytical engine---combining (i) a \emph{Twisted Bochner Monotonicity} identity for the AM-Hawking mass with a twist potential constructed from the Komar form, (ii) a \emph{Conformal Cohomological Mass Comparison} via a twisted Hodge decomposition on the Jang cylinder, (iii) a \emph{Kerr--Cartan Integral Identity} based on Cartan's moving frames, (iv) a \emph{Weyl--Simon Codazzi Calibration Identity} (Theorem~\ref{thm:wscc-main}) that proves the refined Bray--Khuri bound $R_{\bg}\geq 2\Lambda_J$ on rigorous non-perturbative grounds by introducing the Weyl--Simon commutator $\Xi$ and a square-completion identity, (v) a \emph{Maxwell--Simon Electrovac Extension} (Appendix~\ref{app:maxwell-simon}) that introduces the Maxwell--Simon tensor $\mathcal{S}_{EM}$, establishes a Kato decay estimate for the electromagnetic twist potential $\omega_{EM}$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:kato-em}), and yields the full Kerr--Newman AM-Penrose inequality under sub-extremality (Corollary~\ref{cor:full-KN}), and (vi) a \emph{Twisted Callias--Dirac Calibration and Spectral Mass Formula} (Appendix~\ref{app:twisted-dirac}) that introduces a twisted Callias--Dirac operator $\mathcal{D}_{\omega,\Phi}$ with the twist potential as a Higgs field, proves a twisted Lichnerowicz--Weitzenb\"ock identity $\mathcal{D}_{\omega,\Phi}^2 = \nabla^*\nabla + \mathrm{scal}_{\tg}/4 + \Phi_\omega^2 + \gamma(\nabla\Phi_\omega)$, and defines a zeta-regularized spectral mass $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{spec}}(\omega)$ yielding a third independent conditional proof of the AM-Penrose inequality via heat kernel and zeta-function methods (Theorem~\ref{thm:spectral-mass-formula}). Together, these innovations remove the auxiliary monotonicity and mass-comparison inputs (H6)--(H7) and replace them by proven geometric formulae, closing the last remaining analytical gap. The synthesis yields a strengthened AM-Penrose theorem (Theorem~\ref{thm:strengthened-main}) with a complete Kerr rigidity characterization (Theorem~\ref{thm:strong-rigidity}), and the Kerr--Cartan identity and the Spectral Mass Formula provide two independent direct routes that bypass the Jang--conformal--AMO pipeline.
\end{abstract}
