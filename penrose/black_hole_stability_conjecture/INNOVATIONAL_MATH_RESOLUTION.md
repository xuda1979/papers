# Heuristic Mathematical Ideas for Kerr Stability Blockers

This note records speculative mathematical language that may be useful for brainstorming, but it is **not** a proof of the black hole stability conjecture and it does **not** close the analytic gaps identified in the main manuscript. Any item below would have to be replaced by precise definitions, symbol classes, estimates, compatibility checks, and peer-verifiable arguments before it could serve as a theorem.

## 1. The Holomorphic Spectral Stokes Functor (Possible Direction for Gaps A1-A3, B2, C1)

Standard WKB methods and monodromy connections break down due to phase integral branch cuts. One possible research direction is a **Holomorphic Spectral Stokes Functor** $\mathfrak{S}_{\Omega}$ that maps the Teukolsky radial equation into a Riemann surface bundle over the complex frequency plane $\mathbb{C}_{\omega}$.

What would be needed:

- Exact hypotheses under which the construction is defined for the Teukolsky radial equation.
- Quantitative bounds for Stokes multiplier jumps.
- A derivation of the $O(n^{-1})$ correction with controlled remainders.
- A proof that QNM boundary conditions are represented faithfully.
- A separate treatment of superradiant frequency ranges.

## 2. Twisted Thermodynamic Entropy Currents (Possible Direction for Gaps B1, B3, C3)

The derivation of near-extremal limits and heat capacities requires exact geometry limits. One possible research direction is a family of **Twisted Thermodynamic Entropy Currents** designed to track angular momentum gradients in a coordinate-invariant way.

What would be needed:

- A coordinate-invariant definition of the current and measure.
- A comparison with the standard induced measure on $\Sigma_t$.
- A checked calculation of $(\partial T / \partial M)_J$ and the near-extremal limit.

## 3. Coercive Fractional Mode Interpolation (Possible Direction for Gaps C2, D1-D3)

Interpolating between QNM decay and Price tails rigorously is blocked by mode convergence and branch-cut analysis. One possible research direction is a **Fractional Sobolev-Lorentz Interpolation Space** adapted to Kerr mode decompositions.

What would be needed:

- A precise Hilbert or Banach space definition compatible with Kerr mode decompositions.
- A Plancherel or frame theorem with explicit losses.
- Uniform convergence estimates for QNM expansions and branch-cut tails.
- A proof that the resulting decay bound closes the near-extremal bootstrap.

These ideas are therefore research prompts, not resolutions. The stopping rule in the main manuscript still applies: the conjecture is not proved until the required coercive energy, Morawetz, trapped-superradiant positivity, Fourier certificate, and nonlinear gauge/modulation estimates are supplied as actual theorems.
