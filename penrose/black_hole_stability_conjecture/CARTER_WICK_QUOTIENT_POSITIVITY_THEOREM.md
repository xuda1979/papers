# The Carter–Wick Quotient Positivity Theorem and Assembly Budget

## Status

This note invents a new mathematical structure aimed at the trapped-superradiant
sign obstruction in the full-subextremal nonlinear Kerr stability conjecture.
It is **not** an unconditional proof of Kerr stability. The theorem below is
stated precisely, and a machine-checkable certificate format and verifier are
provided alongside it. The verifier certifies that a proposed finite atlas,
local positivity certificates, partition of unity, and derivative-loss budget
satisfy the assembly inequality. Replacing the toy inputs with Kerr-derived
interval data and proved Gram matrices would close the trapped-superradiant
gap *in the linearized sector*, conditional on the standard nonlinear
bootstrap. The remaining nonlinear obstructions (metric reconstruction,
gauge/modulation, final-state identification) are not addressed here.

## 1. The obstruction, restated

Let $K_{a,M}$ be a fixed subextremal Kerr spacetime with $|a|<M$. Let
$P_s$ denote the spin-$s$ Teukolsky operator acting on a separated mode
$\Psi = e^{-i\omega t + i m\phi} S(\theta;\,a\omega) R(r;\,a,M,\omega,\lambda)$.
The principal symbol of the radial transport, in the Carter-separated
variables $(r,\eta,\lambda,\omega)$, takes the form

$$ q_s(r,\eta,\lambda,\omega) \;=\; \Delta\, \partial_r(\Delta\, \partial_r) \;+\; \Phi_s(r,\eta,\lambda,\omega), $$

where $\Delta = r^2 - 2Mr + a^2$, $\eta = Q/E^2$ is the Carter constant
ratio, $\lambda$ is the angular eigenvalue, and $\Phi_s$ is the spin-$s$
potential. The trapped-superradiant obstruction is that on the photon-sphere
region (where the radial effective potential has a nondegenerate maximum) and
in the superradiant interval $\omega \in (m\Omega_H^-,\, m\Omega_H^+)$, the
symbol $q_s$ changes sign, so no global coercive energy is available.

The Carter-calibrated framework of this repository replaces "find a global
energy" by "certify membership of $q_s$ in a finitely generated positive cone
on a finite atlas of the trapped-superradiant torus fibration." The missing
step is the **assembly**: a theorem that local positivity on each chart
propagates to global coercivity modulo an explicitly bounded derivative loss.

## 2. New definitions

### 2.1. The trapped-superradiant quotient ring

Let $\mathfrak V = \mathbb Q[r,\eta,\lambda,\omega]$ be the polynomial ring in
the Carter-separated variables. Let $\mathfrak I_\chi \subset \mathfrak V$ be
the **Carter constraint ideal** generated by the polynomial relations that
hold on the trapped-superradiant torus fibration $\mathcal T_\chi$ at fixed
spin $a/M$. The quotient

$$ \mathfrak A_\chi \;:=\; \mathfrak V / \mathfrak I_\chi $$

is the **trapped-superradiant quotient ring**. Its spectrum
$\mathrm{Spec}(\mathfrak A_\chi) \otimes \mathbb R$ is a finite cover of
$\mathcal T_\chi$.

A polynomial $f \in \mathfrak V$ is **strictly positive on the chart** $U_\alpha
\subset \mathrm{Spec}(\mathfrak A_\chi)$ if there exist rational $c_\alpha >
0$ and $C_\alpha \ge c_\alpha$ such that

$$ c_\alpha \;\le\; f|_{U_\alpha} \;\le\; C_\alpha. $$

### 2.2. Local positivity certificate

A **chart positivity certificate** for the spin-$s$ principal symbol $q_s$ on a
chart $U_\alpha$ is a tuple

$$ \mathcal C_\alpha \;=\; (U_\alpha,\; \mathfrak v_\alpha,\; G_\alpha,\; \mu_\alpha,\; M_\alpha), $$

where:
- $\mathfrak v_\alpha = (v_1, \dots, v_{N_\alpha})$ is a finite monomial
  basis of $\mathfrak A_\chi$ on $U_\alpha$;
- $G_\alpha \in \mathbb Q^{N_\alpha \times N_\alpha}$ is symmetric
  positive-definite with $\|G_\alpha\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le M_\alpha$;
- the **Gram identity** holds in $\mathfrak A_\chi$:

  $$ q_s \;=\; \mathfrak v_\alpha^T\, G_\alpha\, \mathfrak v_\alpha \quad \text{in } \mathfrak A_\chi; $$

- the **local margin** $\mu_\alpha \in \mathbb Q_{>0}$ satisfies

  $$ \mu_\alpha \;\le\; \mathfrak v_\alpha^T\, G_\alpha\, \mathfrak v_\alpha \quad \text{on } U_\alpha. $$

The existence of $\mathcal C_\alpha$ on a single chart is what the existing
`verify_carter_gram_certificate.py` and `verify_carter_chart_certificate.py`
checkers certify.

### 2.3. Bump assembly and overlap multiplicities

Let $\{\chi_\alpha\}$ be a finite partition of unity on
$\mathrm{Spec}(\mathfrak A_\chi) \otimes \mathbb R$ subordinate to the atlas
$\{U_\alpha\}$. Each $\chi_\alpha$ is a smooth bump with support in
$U_\alpha$. Define:

- the **overlap multiplicity** $m_\alpha := \sum_\beta \mathbf 1\{\mathrm{supp}\, \chi_\alpha \cap \mathrm{supp}\, \chi_\beta \ne \varnothing\}$;
- the **bump width** $w_\alpha > 0$, the inradius of $\mathrm{supp}\, \chi_\alpha$ in the chart coordinates;
- the **bump constant** $C_{\mathrm{bump}} \in \mathbb Q_{>0}$ such that
  $\sup_\alpha \|\nabla \chi_\alpha\|_{L^\infty} \le C_{\mathrm{bump}}\, w_\alpha^{-1}$ and
  $\sup_\alpha \|\Delta \chi_\alpha\|_{L^\infty} \le C_{\mathrm{bump}}\, w_\alpha^{-2}$.

### 2.4. The derivative-loss budget

The assembly of the chart Gram identities into a global commutator estimate
incurs derivative loss from (i) differentiating the bumps $\chi_\alpha$ and
(ii) integrating the local margins $\mu_\alpha$ against the partition of unity.
We define the **per-chart derivative loss**

$$ \mathrm{Loss}_\alpha \;:=\; \frac{M_\alpha}{\mu_\alpha}\,\bigl(1 + C_{\mathrm{bump}}\, w_\alpha^{-1}\bigr), $$

and the **total derivative loss**

$$ \mathrm{Loss}_{\mathrm{tot}} \;:=\; \sum_\alpha \mathrm{Loss}_\alpha. $$

The **available derivative buffer** $B \in \mathbb Q_{>0}$ is the total
high-frequency energy slack left after the standard $r^p$-weighted estimate
and the redshift estimate of Dafermos–Rodnianski are applied. The assembly
closes if $\mathrm{Loss}_{\mathrm{tot}} \le B$.

## 3. Theorem (Carter–Wick Quotient Positivity with Assembly Budget)

**Theorem (CWQP-AB, stated).** *Let $K_{a,M}$ be a fixed subextremal Kerr
spacetime with $|a|<M$. Let $\mathfrak A_\chi$ be the trapped-superradiant
quotient ring at fixed spin. Suppose:*

1. *(Atlas)* *There is a finite atlas $\{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha=1}^{N}$ of
   $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathfrak A_\chi) \otimes \mathbb R$ with explicit rational
   endpoints and overlap multiplicities $\{m_\alpha\}$.*
2. *(Local positivity)* *On each $U_\alpha$ there is a chart positivity
   certificate $\mathcal C_\alpha = (U_\alpha, \mathfrak v_\alpha, G_\alpha,
   \mu_\alpha, M_\alpha)$ for the spin-$s$ principal symbol $q_s$.*
3. *(Bump assembly)* *There is a partition of unity $\{\chi_\alpha\}$
   subordinate to $\{U_\alpha\}$ with bump widths $\{w_\alpha\}$ and bump
   constant $C_{\mathrm{bump}}$.*
4. *(Budget closure)* *With $B$ the available derivative buffer,*

   $$ \sum_{\alpha=1}^{N} \frac{M_\alpha}{\mu_\alpha}\,\bigl(1 + C_{\mathrm{bump}}\, w_\alpha^{-1}\bigr) \;\le\; B. $$

*Then the spin-$s$ linearized Teukolsky equation on the exterior of $K_{a,M}$
admits a coercive positive-commutator estimate*

$$ \int_{\Sigma_\tau} \mathfrak T_s[\Psi_s]\, d\mu \;\ge\; c_s \int_{\Sigma_\tau} |\nabla \Psi_s|^2\, d\mu \;-\; \mathrm{Err}_s[\Psi_s], $$

*with $c_s > 0$ depending only on $\min_\alpha \mu_\alpha / m_\alpha$, and the
error term $\mathrm{Err}_s$ absorbing a finite sum of lower-order Sobolev
norms with total coefficient bounded by $\mathrm{Loss}_{\mathrm{tot}} \le B$.*

### 3.1. Why this is the right theorem

The existing framework reduces the trapped-superradiant sign problem to local
SOS-on-quotient certificates. The missing step is a **quantitative assembly
theorem** that converts local positivity into a global coercive estimate with
explicit derivative-loss budget. The CWQP-AB theorem is precisely that
assembly step. Its hypotheses are:

- finitely checkable (atlas, local Gram, bump widths, budget inequality);
- rational-arithmetic compatible (every constant is rational);
- modular (each chart certificate is independently checkable).

The conclusion is the standard positive-commutator estimate required by the
linearized sector of the Klainerman–Szeftel / Giorgi–Klainerman–Szeftel
nonlinear bootstrap, with the derivative loss explicitly bounded by the
budget inequality (4).

### 3.2. What is genuinely new

1. **Quotient-ring formulation.** The positivity statement is in
   $\mathfrak A_\chi = \mathbb Q[r,\eta,\lambda,\omega]/\mathfrak I_\chi$
   rather than in the ambient polynomial ring. This makes the Carter
   constraints first-class: positivity modulo the constraint ideal is a
   weaker and more honest requirement than ambient positivity.

2. **Explicit assembly budget.** The derivative-loss inequality (4) is
   quantitative and rational. It replaces the usual qualitative "the
   commutator estimate closes for small enough perturbations" by a
   checkable inequality on the chart data.

3. **Decoupling of local and global.** Local positivity (per-chart Gram
   certificates, already implemented) and global assembly (the new theorem)
   are decoupled. This matches the existing verifier architecture and
   permits independent certification.

## 4. Proof sketch (conditional on the hypotheses)

The proof is a quantitative version of the standard positive-commutator
argument with a partition of unity.

**Step 1 (local commutator).** On each chart $U_\alpha$, the Gram identity
$q_s = \mathfrak v_\alpha^T G_\alpha \mathfrak v_\alpha$ in
$\mathfrak A_\chi$ together with the local margin $\mu_\alpha \le
\mathfrak v_\alpha^T G_\alpha \mathfrak v_\alpha$ gives, after quantization
through the Carter–Wick symbol calculus of this repository, a local
commutator estimate

$$ \langle [P_s, A_\alpha]\, \Psi_s, \Psi_s \rangle_{U_\alpha}
   \;\ge\; \mu_\alpha\, \|\Psi_s\|^2_{H^1(U_\alpha)} \;-\; M_\alpha\, \|\Psi_s\|^2_{L^2(U_\alpha)}, $$

where $A_\alpha$ is the chart-local self-adjoint pseudodifferential operator
with symbol $\mathfrak v_\alpha^T G_\alpha \mathfrak v_\alpha$. The ratio
$M_\alpha/\mu_\alpha$ is the local loss.

**Step 2 (bump localization).** Multiply $A_\alpha$ by the bump
$\chi_\alpha$. By the Wick-symbol composition rule (which holds in the
Carter-separated calculus because the symplectic form is diagonal in
$(r,\eta,\lambda,\omega)$),

$$ [P_s, \chi_\alpha A_\alpha \chi_\alpha]
   \;=\; \chi_\alpha^2\, [P_s, A_\alpha]
   \;+\; 2\, \mathrm{Im}(H_{q_s}\, \chi_\alpha)\, A_\alpha
   \;+\; \mathrm{lower\ order}. $$

The Hamiltonian vector field $H_{q_s}$ is bounded by $\|G_\alpha\|_{\mathrm{op}}\, \|\mathfrak v_\alpha\|^2 \le M_\alpha$ on $U_\alpha$. The bump derivatives contribute $C_{\mathrm{bump}}\, w_\alpha^{-1}\, M_\alpha$. Hence

$$ \langle [P_s, \chi_\alpha A_\alpha \chi_\alpha]\, \Psi_s, \Psi_s \rangle
   \;\ge\; \mu_\alpha\, \|\chi_\alpha \Psi_s\|^2_{H^1}
   \;-\; \mathrm{Loss}_\alpha\, \|\Psi_s\|^2_{L^2(\mathrm{supp}\, \chi_\alpha)}. $$

**Step 3 (assembly).** Sum over $\alpha$. Using $\sum_\alpha \chi_\alpha^2 = 1$
and the overlap multiplicity bound $\sum_\alpha \mathbf 1_{\mathrm{supp}\, \chi_\alpha} \le \max_\alpha m_\alpha$, we obtain

$$ \langle [P_s, A]\, \Psi_s, \Psi_s \rangle
   \;\ge\; \frac{\min_\alpha \mu_\alpha}{\max_\alpha m_\alpha}\, \|\Psi_s\|^2_{H^1}
   \;-\; \mathrm{Loss}_{\mathrm{tot}}\, \|\Psi_s\|^2_{L^2}, $$

where $A := \sum_\alpha \chi_\alpha A_\alpha \chi_\alpha$ is the global
commutant. The hypothesis $\mathrm{Loss}_{\mathrm{tot}} \le B$ ensures the
$L^2$ error is absorbed by the available derivative buffer (a standard
redshift-plus-$r^p$ argument of Dafermos–Rodnianski type).

**Step 4 (coercivity).** The standard elliptic estimate for $P_s$ on the
exterior, combined with Step 3, yields the stated coercive inequality with
$c_s = \min_\alpha \mu_\alpha / \max_\alpha m_\alpha$. $\square$

### 4.1. What the proof sketch does *not* supply

The proof sketch assumes:
- the Carter–Wick symbol calculus on $\mathfrak A_\chi$ with the stated
  composition rule and bounded remainder;
- the redshift-plus-$r^p$ absorption of the $L^2$ error in Step 3;
- a uniform Sobolev embedding compatible with the chart localization.

These are open proof obligations. The verifier below checks the **combinatorial
and budget** content of the theorem (hypotheses 1–4); it does not check the
analytic Step 1–4 sketch.

## 5. Machine-checkable certificate format

A CWQP-AB certificate is a JSON object with the following fields:

- `metadata`: name, generated_on, spin, claim_scope, status, warning.
- `atlas`: list of charts with `name`, `endpoints`, `width`, `overlap_multiplicity`.
- `bump`: object with `bump_constant` (rational) and `bump_type`.
- `charts`: list of per-chart positivity certificates, each with
  `name`, `monomial_basis_size`, `gram_op_norm_bound` (rational),
  `local_margin` (rational), and reference to the underlying Gram certificate.
- `derivative_buffer`: rational $B$.
- `budget`: per-chart `loss` (rational) and `total_loss` (rational).
- `closure`: boolean `total_loss <= derivative_buffer`.

The verifier `verify_quotient_positivity_assembly.py` checks:
1. every required field is present and well-typed;
2. every rational is a valid `Fraction` representation;
3. the local margin $\mu_\alpha$ and Gram op-norm bound $M_\alpha$ are positive;
4. the per-chart loss is computed correctly as
   $M_\alpha / \mu_\alpha \cdot (1 + C_{\mathrm{bump}} / w_\alpha)$;
5. the total loss is the sum of per-chart losses;
6. the closure inequality $\mathrm{Loss}_{\mathrm{tot}} \le B$ holds;
7. the atlas covers a contiguous interval (gap-free);
8. the overlap multiplicities are consistent with the atlas adjacencies.

## 6. What this would buy us

If the toy inputs in the certificate were replaced by:
- the proved Carter quotient ideal $\mathfrak I_\chi$ for fixed $|a|<M$;
- proved per-chart Gram matrices $G_\alpha$ with rational entries (the
  existing `solve_carter_gram_certificate.py` produces these on small
  monomial bases);
- a proved bump construction with explicit $C_{\mathrm{bump}}$ and $w_\alpha$;
- a proved $r^p$ + redshift derivative buffer $B$ for the spin-$s$
  Teukolsky equation at fixed $|a|<M$;

then the CWQP-AB theorem would close the **trapped-superradiant linear
sign obstruction** for the spin-$s$ linearized Teukolsky equation on the
exterior of fixed subextremal Kerr. The remaining nonlinear obstructions
(metric reconstruction, gauge/modulation, final-state identification) are
the ones listed in `current_public_status_package.json` and are not
addressed here.

## 7. Honest stopping rule

This theorem and its verifier are a **structural reduction**, not a proof.
The conjecture is not solved until:
- the Carter–Wick symbol calculus on $\mathfrak A_\chi$ is constructed with
  the stated composition rule and remainder bound (Step 2 of the sketch);
- the redshift-plus-$r^p$ absorption in Step 3 is proved with the stated
  uniformity;
- the nonlinear bootstrap of Giorgi–Klainerman–Szeftel type is completed
  with this linear estimate as input.

The verifier accepts only certificates that explicitly bound the
derivative-loss budget, so any premature "proof" claim that ignores the
budget is rejected by construction.
