\subsection{Status of the Computer-Assisted Proof (formal assumptions)}
\label{sec:conditional-status}
The proof in this manuscript is a \textbf{Computer-Assisted Proof (CAP)}: it does not assume an \emph{unproven physical ansatz} (e.g. a particular vacuum structure), but it \emph{does} rely on explicitly stated computational hypotheses standard in CAP work:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The mathematical correctness of the algorithms implemented in the verification code (e.g. \texttt{rigorous\_constants\_derivation.py} and \texttt{full\_verifier\_phase2.py}).
\item The faithful execution of the interval-arithmetic kernel and its dependency stack.
\item The usual floating-point arithmetic model (IEEE 754) assumed by the runtime environment.
\end{enumerate}
Under these computational assumptions (which are made auditable and reproducible in Appendix~K and the \texttt{verification/} bundle), the certified inequalities imply the claimed mass gap.

\subsection{Limitations and Open Problems}

While explicitly constructive, our proof relies on specific technical assumptions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Uniqueness of the Continuum Limit}: We prove the \textit{existence} of a continuum limit with a mass gap via subsequential convergence (Theorem 17.1.28). The rigorous proof of the \textit{uniqueness} of this limit is left as an open problem, though Universality suggests it is unique.
    \item \textbf{Global Lorentz Restoration}: The restoration of Lorentz invariance relies on the non-singularity of the restoration map Jacobian, which is rigorously verified only within the "Tube" of the CAP. We assume no singularities exist outside this verified domain that could obstruct the global existence of the trajectory.
    \item \textbf{Gribov Ambiguity}: In the non-perturbative regime, we restrict the path integral to the "Fundamental Modular Domain" to handle Gribov copies (Appendix D). While standard in lattice theory, fully rigorous infinite-volume control of the Gribov horizon remains a challenge in constructive QFT.
\end{enumerate}
