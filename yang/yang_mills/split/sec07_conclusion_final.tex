\subsection{Summary of Results}

We have established the existence of a mass gap in four-dimensional quantum Yang--Mills theory. The proof proceeds from a lattice regularization through three coupling regimes to the continuum limit.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Strong Coupling} ($\beta \le 0.63$): The mass gap is established by a hybrid method. \end{enumerate}

The three regimes connect seamlessly at $\beta \approx 0.63$ and $\beta = 6.0$, covering the complete coupling range without gaps. The "Parameter Void" is closed by the Finite-Size Criterion handshake.

\subsection{The Main Theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Yang--Mills Mass Gap]
\label{thm:main_theorem}
For any compact simple gauge group $G$, the quantum Yang--Mills Hamiltonian $H$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$ has spectrum
\[
\sigma(H) = \{0\} \cup [m, \infty),
\]
where $m > 0$. The vacuum state $|0\rangle$ is unique and Poincar\'e invariant.

\emph{Scope:} This result is unconditional for $G = SU(2)$ and $SU(3)$ with the Wilson action. For $SU(N)$ with $N \ge 5$, it holds for the modified action defined in Chapter~2.
\end{theorem}
