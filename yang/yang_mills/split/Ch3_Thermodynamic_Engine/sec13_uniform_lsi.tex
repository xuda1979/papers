\begin{proof}

Let $H_k = \nabla^2 S_k$ be the Hessian of the effective action at scale $k$. We prove by induction that $H_k$ is bounded below uniformly on the tangent space of the gauge orbit quotient:
\[ H_k \ge m_k \mathbb{I} \]

\begin{itemize}

\[ m_{k+1} \ge \lambda^2 m_k - O(e^{-m_{loc} L}) \]
