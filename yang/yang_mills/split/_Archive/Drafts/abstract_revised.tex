We present a rigorous review of the current status of the mass gap problem in 4-dimensional Yang-Mills theory. We consolidate known results from lattice gauge theory, specifically the rigorous control of the strong coupling regime via cluster expansions. We then analyze the obstruction to extending these results to the continuum limit. We discuss a proposed strategy involving an interpolation to Supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory via massive adjoint fermions, identifying the specific "Adiabatic Continuity" conjecture required for this approach to succeed. We clarify the analytic requirements for a complete proof, distinguishing between established theorems and necessary open inputs regarding phase transitions and non-perturbative renormalization group flow.
