\section{Strong Coupling Regime}
\label{sec:strong_coupling}

In the strong coupling limit ($g \gg 1$, or small $\beta$), the theory is controlled by the Cluster Expansion.

\subsection{Character Expansion}
We expand the Boltzmann weight in terms of irreducible characters of $SU(N)$:
\begin{equation}
e^{\frac{\beta}{N} \text{Re Tr} U} = c_0(\beta) \left( 1 + \sum_{r \neq 0} d_r a_r(\beta) \chi_r(U) \right)
\end{equation}
Integrating over link variables generates a series of graphs ("polymers") consisting of induced plaquettes.

\subsection{Convergence of the Cluster Expansion}
The convergence of this series is governed by the Koteck\'y-Preiss condition.
\begin{theorem}[Convergence at Strong Coupling]
There exists a critical coupling $\beta_c > 0$ such that for all $\beta < \beta_c$, the cluster expansion for the free energy density and correlation functions converges absolutely.
\end{theorem}
This result relies on standard combinatorial estimates (number of polymers of size $L$ growing exponentially, suppressed by weight $O(\beta)^L$).

\subsection{Mass Gap}
As a consequence of the convergence of the cluster expansion:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The two-point correlation function decays exponentially:
    \begin{equation}
    \langle \text{Tr} U_p(0) \text{Tr} U_p(x) \rangle_c \le C e^{-m(\beta) |x|}
    \end{equation}
    \item The mass gap $m(\beta)$ is strictly positive:
    \begin{equation}
    m(\beta) \sim -\ln(\beta) \quad \text{as } \beta \to 0
    \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}
This provides a rigorous proof of the mass gap in the strong coupling regime. The difficulty, and the core of the Millennium Problem, is to extend this result to $\beta \to \infty$ (Continuum Limit).
