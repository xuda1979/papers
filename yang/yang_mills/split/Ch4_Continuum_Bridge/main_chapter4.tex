\chapter*{Chapter Objectives}
The primary objective of this chapter is to construct the continuum limit framework and analyze the stability in the ultraviolet regime. Specifically, we aim to establish:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Theorem \ref{thm:rigorous-resolvent-limit}:} The existence of a valid physical Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_{phys}$ and a positive Hamiltonian $H \ge 0$ in the limit $a \to 0$ (weak coupling $\beta \to \infty$), under the assumption of spectral permanence.
    \item \textbf{Methodology:} This relies on Balaban's Renormalization Group analysis to control the effective action and ensure the theory remains in the domain of attraction of the Gaussian fixed point.
\end{enumerate}
This chapter connects the uniform lattice theory from Chapter III to the physical continuum theory.
