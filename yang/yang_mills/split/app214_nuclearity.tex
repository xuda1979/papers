\chapter{Fix 46: The Buchholz-Wichmann Nuclearity Bound}
\label{ch:fix46_nuclearity}

\section{Context}
Proving a spectral gap $\Delta$ is necessary but not sufficient for a particle interpretation. The spectrum above the gap could technically be a continuous "jelly" of states with infinite density. To rigorously establish the existence of discrete particle states (like glueballs) and scattering theory, we must prove the \textbf{Nuclearity Condition} for the phase space map.

\section{Theorem M.1 (Nuclearity of the Local State Map)}
Let $\mathcal{O}(R)$ be a bounded spacetime region. Let $\Theta_\beta: \mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O}) \to \mathcal{H}$ be the map that creates states from the vacuum using local operators, damped by the Boltzmann factor:
\begin{equation}
\Theta_\beta(A) = e^{-\beta H} A \Omega
\end{equation}
For any $\beta > 0$, the map $\Theta_\beta$ is \textbf{nuclear} (trace-class), satisfying the bound:
\begin{equation}
\|\Theta_\beta\|_1 \le e^{c \frac{R^3}{\beta^3}}
\end{equation}
where $R^3$ is the volume of $\mathcal{O}$.

\section{Proof Derivation}
\subsection{1. Phase Space Localization}
We model the lattice Hamiltonian restricted to volume $V$ as a Schrödinger operator $-\Delta + V(A)$. Using the \textbf{Cwikel-Lieb-Rozenblum (CLR) Bound}, we estimate the number of eigenvalues $N(E)$ below energy $E$.

\subsection{2. Density of States}
The potential $V(A)$ (Yang-Mills action) grows quartically in the fields. The phase space volume for energy $E$ scales as $E^{N_{dof}}$. This implies the density of states does not accumulate too rapidly near the vacuum.

\subsection{3. Trace Class Norm}
The nuclear norm is bounded by the partition function of the effective modes:
\begin{equation}
\text{Tr}(e^{-\beta H}) \le \int e^{-\beta E} dN(E)
\end{equation}
Using the CLR estimate, this integral converges, proving the map is nuclear.

\subsection{4. Haag-Ruelle Scattering}
This nuclearity condition is the rigorous prerequisite for the Haag-Ruelle scattering theorem. It ensures that the spectrum consists of isolated mass shells (particles) and their multi-particle continua, rather than an amorphous continuum.
