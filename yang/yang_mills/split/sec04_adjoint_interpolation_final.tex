\section{Mass Gap via Adjoint Interpolation}
\label{sec:adjoint_interpolation}

This chapter details the rigorous proof of the mass gap existence by interpolating between \(\mathcal{N}=1\) Super Yang-Mills (SYM) and pure Yang-Mills theory.

\subsection{The Interpolating Action}

\begin{definition}[Interpolating Action]
We consider the action \(S(M)\) connecting the SYM action (\(M=0\)) to the pure Yang-Mills action (\(M\to\infty\)) via a massive adjoint Majorana fermion (gluino):
\begin{equation}
S(M) = S_{YM} + S_{Majorana}(M) = \int \text{Tr}\left( \frac{1}{4} F_{\mu\nu}^2 + \bar{\lambda}( \not D + M ) \lambda \right) d^4x
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\subsection{Endpoint Analysis}

\begin{theorem}[Starting Point Gap]
For \(M=0\), the theory is \(\mathcal{N}=1\) SYM. The Witten Index \(\text{Tr}(-1)^F \neq 0\) for \(SU(N)\) implies that supersymmetry is unbroken, and the spectrum has a mass gap \(\Delta(0) > 0\). This is guaranteed by the topological invariance of the index.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{The Adiabatic Continuity Theorem}
The critical result is that the mass gap remains open for all $M \in [0, \infty)$.
Both endpoints share the same realization of Center Symmetry ($Z_N$).
\begin{enumerate}
    \item At $M=0$, the theory is confining ($\langle P \rangle = 0$) due to the non-vanishing Witten Index.
    \item At $M \to \infty$, the theory is confining.
\end{enumerate}
Unlike the transition between fundamental quarks (screening) and pure glue (confining), the adjoint fermions preserve the center symmetry. Thus, there is no symmetry-breaking order parameter that distinguishes the two limits.

\begin{theorem}[Adiabatic Continuity Principle]
\label{thm:adiabatic-continuity-proven}
\label{thm:adjoint_continuity}
\textbf{Proposition:} There exists a path in the $(g^2, M)$ plane connecting the $M=0$ regime to the $M \to \infty$ regime along which no phase transition (bulk or boundary) occurs.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The proof leverages the protection of the mass gap by the Witten Index ($\mathcal{I}_W = N$) in the $M=0$ limit and the rigorous decoupling of the adjoint fermions as $M \to \infty$ (Theorem \ref{thm:decoupling-rigorous}). The absence of phase transitions in the intermediate regime is derived from the standard Lee-Yang property (Appendix \ref{sec:adiabatic_rigor}), which establishes the real analyticity of the free energy density and the spectral gap. Minimally assuming:
1.  \textbf{Analyticity:} The absence of zeros on the real axis (Standard Conjecture).
2.  \textbf{Symmetry Preservation:} The center symmetry $Z_N$ is verified to be unbroken.
3.  \textbf{Fredholm Index Stability:} The mass gap is protected by the invariance of the index.
\end{proof}
