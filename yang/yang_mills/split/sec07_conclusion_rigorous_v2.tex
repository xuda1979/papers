\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The problem of proving the existence of a mass gap in four-dimensional Yang-Mills theory remains one of the most challenging in mathematical physics. In this manuscript, we have:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Reviewed the rigorous lattice formulation and the existence of the gap in finite volume and strong coupling.
    \item Clarified the distinction between the proven regimes (Strong Coupling) and the open regimes (Continuum Limit).
    \item Outlined the "Adjoint Interpolation" strategy as a potential roadmap for establishing the mass gap, while explicitly identifying the "Adiabatic Continuity" conjecture as the key missing link.
\end{enumerate}

Future progress requires a breakthrough in non-perturbative analysis to control the continuum limit without relying on unproven continuity assumptions. The frameworks discussed here provide a clear map of the current state of knowledge and the precise technical obstacles that must be overcome to claim a solution to the Millennium Prize Problem.
