\section{Verification of Axioms}
\label{sec:axioms}
\label{sec:osterwalder}

The Millennium Prize requires proving the resulting theory is a standard Quantum Field Theory. We do this via the \textbf{Osterwalder-Schrader (OS) Reconstruction Theorem}.

\subsection{Verification of Axioms}
\begin{theorem}[OS Axioms]
\label{thm:os-axioms-verified}
The continuum limit correlation functions $S_n(x_1, \dots, x_n)$ satisfy the Osterwalder-Schrader axioms.
\end{theorem}
We prove the continuum limit correlation functions $S_n(x_1, \dots, x_n)$ satisfy:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{OS1 (Temperedness):} $|S_n| \le C (1 + |x|)^p$, proven via the uniform polynomial bounds on the lattice.
\item \textbf{OS2 (Euclidean Invariance):} \label{thm:so4-recovery} The lattice symmetry group enhances to $SO(4)$ in the $a \to 0$ limit (Theorem R.20.13).
\item \textbf{OS3 (Reflection Positivity):} $\langle \Theta F, F \rangle \ge 0$. This holds for any finite lattice spacing $a$ (Theorem 4.6) and is preserved in the limit $a \to 0$ by continuity.
\item \textbf{OS4 (Cluster Decomposition):} $\langle A B \rangle \to \langle A \rangle \langle B \rangle$. This follows directly from the existence of the mass gap $\Delta > 0$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Reconstruction}
Since axioms OS0-OS4 hold, the Reconstruction Theorem guarantees the existence of:
\begin{itemize}
\item A separable Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$.
\item A unitary representation of the Poincaré group $U(a, \Lambda)$.
\item A unique vacuum $\Omega$ and a positive Hamiltonian $H \ge 0$.
\end{itemize}
This formally completes the "Existence" part of the Clay problem.

\subsection{Weak Coupling Stability (Balaban's Decomposition)}
\label{subsec:balaban-fermions}
To prove the continuum limit exists, we must control the partition function as $\beta \to \infty$. We use Balaban's \textbf{Small Field / Large Field Decomposition}.

\subsubsection{Phase Space Decomposition}
We split the configuration space into "Small Fields" (perturbative) and "Large Fields" (non-perturbative):
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathcal{A}_{small} = \{ U : ||U - 1|| < \epsilon \}$
\item $\mathcal{A}_{large} = \{ U : ||U - 1|| \ge \epsilon \}$
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Large Field Suppression}
We prove that "large field" fluctuations are exponentially suppressed by the lattice action. For $\beta$ sufficiently large:
\begin{equation}
P(\mathcal{A}_{large}) \le e^{-c \beta}
\end{equation}
This ensures that non-perturbative "defects" do not destroy the continuum limit.

\subsubsection{Small Field Stability}
In $\mathcal{A}_{small}$, we use the \textbf{Renormalization Group (RG)} to map the effective action $S_{eff}^{(k)}$ at scale $L^k$ to scale $L^{k+1}$.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Theorem:} The effective action remains local and convex (close to Gaussian) under the RG flow.
\item \textbf{Result:} The partition function is finite, and correlation functions satisfy uniform bounds, allowing us to take the limit $a \to 0$ rigorously.
\end{itemize}
