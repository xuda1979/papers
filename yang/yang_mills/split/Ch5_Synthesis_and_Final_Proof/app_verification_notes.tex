\section{Verification Components}

The suite consists of three primary modules that map directly to the logic of the proof:

\subsection{1. Geometric Foundation (\texttt{rigorous\_constants\_derivation.py})}
\label{sec:geometric-constants}
This module implements the \emph{ab initio} derivation of the bounding constants used in the proof. It answers the "Circularity" critique by deriving the \textbf{Pollution Constant} $C_{poll}$ using strictly geometric arguments (surface-to-volume scaling on the hypercube) and worst-case polynomial decay assumptions.

\textbf{Key Result (Section \ref{sec:geometric-constants}):}
\begin{equation}
    C_{poll} \le C_{geo} \cdot C_{decay} \cdot C_{mix} \approx 0.02
\end{equation}
This constant ensures that the feedback from relevant operators into the irrelevant tail is suppressed by a factor of 50 relative to the stability threshold.

\subsection{2. The Flow Engine (\texttt{full\_verifier\_phase2.py})}
This script executes the iterative step of the Renormalization Group. For each step $k$ from $\beta=\VerBetaIntermediateMin$ to $\beta=\VerBetaIntermediateMax$:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Constructs the "Tube" $T_k$ as a ball in the Banach space of effective actions.
    \item Computes the image $T'_{k+1} = \mathcal{R}(T_k)$ using Interval Arithmetic.
    \item Verifies the inclusion $T'_{k+1} \subset T_{k+1}$ with explicit safety margins.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{3. Spectral Analysis (\texttt{ab\_initio\_jacobian.py})}
Computes the eigenvalues of the linearized RG map around the trivial fixed point to determine the contraction rates $\lambda_{rel}$ and $\lambda_{irr}$.

\section{Data Persistence}

All verification runs generate a cryptographic hash of the input parameters and the output logs. The files \texttt{rigorous\_constants.json} and \texttt{certificate\_phase2\_hardened.json} included in the repository serve as the permanent record of the proof's numerical components.
