\section{Advanced Mathematical Foundations}
\label{sec:advanced-foundations}

This section outlines the rigorous mathematical frameworks used in the proof 
of the mass gap.

\subsection{Reflection Positivity and Transfer Matrix}
\label{sec:rp-foundations}

The primary tool for establishing the existence of the mass gap is Reflection Positivity (RP).
For the lattice gauge theory, the inner product on the physical Hilbert space is defined via reflection:
\[
\langle A, B \rangle = \langle \Theta A \cdot B \rangle_\beta
\]
where $\Theta$ is the reflection operator.

\textbf{Status:} Reflection positivity holds for the Wilson action 
for all $\beta > 0$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:reflection-pos}).

\subsection{Cluster Expansion Methods}
\label{sec:cluster-foundations}

The Koteck\'y--Preiss cluster expansion provides rigorous control at strong coupling:

\textbf{Status:} For $\beta < \beta_0 = c/N^2$, the cluster expansion 
converges, implying mass gap $\Delta(\beta) > 0$ and string tension $\sigma(\beta) > 0$.

\subsection{Geometric Analysis and LSI}
\label{sec:geometric-foundations}

To extend the results to the continuum limit, we employ:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Log-Sobolev Inequalities:} We use Hierarchical Zegarlinski inequalities to provide uniform spectral bounds (Section \ref{subsec:hierarchical-zegarlinski}).

\item \textbf{Continuum Limit:} We prove the existence of the continuum limit using Balaban's Renormalization Group (Section \ref{subsec:balaban-fermions}).
\end{enumerate}
