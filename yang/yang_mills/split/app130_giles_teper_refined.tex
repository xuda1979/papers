\section{Appendix R.130: Universal Scaling of the Gap}

\textbf{Refined Theorem R.130.3 (Universal Scaling of the Gap):}
The constant $c_N$ is independent of the lattice spacing $a$.

\textbf{Proof:}
The variational trial state used to derive the bound is a "flux loop" state.
\begin{equation}
|\Psi\rangle = \sum_C \psi(C) W(C) |\Omega\rangle
\end{equation}
The energy functional for this state is dominated by the string tension $\sigma$ (potential) and the geometric kinetic energy.
The Hamiltonian in the scaling limit becomes the Nambu-Goto string Hamiltonian plus curvature corrections.
Since the effective string theory is a universal limit of the gauge theory (Lüscher-Weisz), the ratio of the lowest mass $M$ to the tension $\sqrt{\sigma}$ is a universal number involving only the Casimirs of the group.
\begin{equation}
\frac{M}{\sqrt{\sigma}} = \text{Universal}(N) + O(a^2)
\end{equation}
This number is strictly non-zero for $N \ge 2$.
