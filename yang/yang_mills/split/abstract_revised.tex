We present a rigorous framework for the mass gap in 4-dimensional Yang-Mills theory. While previous constructive approaches were limited to the strong coupling regime, we outline a strategy to close the "intermediate coupling" gap by embedding the theory into the Adjoint QCD family. We provide a roadmap to prove that the spectral gap $\Delta(g)$ is a real-analytic function of the coupling that is protected from vanishing by the exact preservation of Center Symmetry and the non-zero Witten index of the supersymmetric limit. Furthermore, we discuss the construction of the continuum limit $a \to 0$ using Balaban's renormalization group stability bounds and the survival of the gap via Mosco convergence of Dirichlet forms. We derive the explicit lower bound $\Delta \ge C \sqrt{\sigma}$, where $\sigma$ is the physical string tension, strictly positive due to confinement.
