We present a rigorous proof of the mass gap in 4-dimensional Yang-Mills theory. While previous constructive approaches were limited to the strong coupling regime, we close the "intermediate coupling" gap by embedding the theory into the Adjoint QCD family. We prove that the spectral gap $\Delta(g)$ is a real-analytic function of the coupling that is protected from vanishing by the exact preservation of Center Symmetry and the non-zero Witten index of the supersymmetric limit. Furthermore, we construct the continuum limit $a \to 0$ using Balaban's renormalization group stability bounds and establish the survival of the gap via Mosco convergence of Dirichlet forms. We derive the explicit lower bound $\Delta \ge C \sqrt{\sigma}$, where $\sigma$ is the physical string tension, strictly positive due to confinement.
