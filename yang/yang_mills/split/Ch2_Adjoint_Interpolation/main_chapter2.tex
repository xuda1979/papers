\chapter*{Chapter Objectives}
The primary objective of this chapter is to physically validate the interpolation between the strong and weak coupling regimes. Specifically, we aim to rigorously prove:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Theorem \ref{thm:no-phase-transitions}:} The absence of phase transitions (singularities in the free energy density) along the interpolation path from the strong coupling (Chapter I) to the weak coupling (Chapter IV) regimes.
    \item \textbf{Methodology:} We utilize the Adjoint Lattice strategy and the Witten Index (Topological Invariant) to prevent gap collapse during the crossover.
\end{enumerate}
This chapter acts as the ``Guard-Rail'' ensuring that the mathematical continuum limit corresponds to the confining phase of Yang-Mills theory.
