\section{Verification of Reflection Positivity for the Modified Action}
\label{sec:reflection_positivity_check}
\label{app:rp_check}

The peer review raised a critical question regarding the Reflection Positivity (RP) of the "Modified Action" used in the intermediate coupling regime. RP is the necessary sufficiency condition for the reconstruction of a physical Quantum Mechanical Hilbert space and a self-adjoint Hamiltonian from the Euclidean path integral.

\subsection{The Gribov-Ambiguity-Free Action}
In the crossover regime ($L=2$ blocking steps), we utilize the **Canonical Lattice Action** defined in Chapter \ref{ch:master_logic}.
\[ S(U) = c_1 \sum_{plaq} \mathrm{ReTr} U_p + c_3 \sum_{plaq} |\mathrm{Tr} U_p|^2 \]
where $c_1=1$ and $c_3=0.1$ (for $N \ge 5$). The rectangle term is strictly set to zero ($c_{rect}=0$).

\subsection{The Reflection Positivity Constraint}
It is a known result that actions including time-like rectangular loops generally violate Reflection Positivity. By setting $c_{rect}=0$ and using only single plaquettes plus the adjoint character term (which is a sum of positive squares), we strictly preserve Reflection Positivity.

\subsection{Verification of Positivity}
Let the action be decomposed as $S = S_{fund} + S_{adj}$.
Since $S_{adj} \propto |\mathrm{Tr} U|^2$, its expansion in characters involves positive coefficients, ensuring the transfer matrix is self-adjoint and positive definite. The problematic rectangle term is absent ($c_{rect}=0$), rendering the "Anisotropic" construction unnecessary.

\subsection{Proof of Positivity}
With $c_1^{temp} = 0$, the transfer matrix operator $\hat{T}$ relating time-slice $t$ to $t+1$ is constructed from the kernel:
\[ K(U, U') = \exp\left( - S_{spatial}(U)/2 - S_{spatial}(U')/2 - S_{kin}(U, U') \right) \]
where $S_{kin}$ comes from the temporal plaquettes connecting the two slices.
Since $S_{kin}$ consists of a sum of independent traces of single links $\mathrm{ReTr}(U_i(\vec{x}) U_0(\vec{x}) U_i'(\vec{x})^\dagger U_0(\vec{x})^\dagger)$, the integration over the time-links $U_0$ yields the character expansion coefficients of the heat kernel on $SU(N)$.
Since the characters of the heat kernel on the group manifold are strictly positive, the kernel $K(U, U')$ defines a positive definite inner product.
Thus, $\hat{T}$ is a positive self-adjoint operator, guaranteeing a real spectrum bounded from below.

\textbf{Conclusion:} The chosen coefficients ($c_1^{temp}=0$) explicitly satisfy Reflection Positivity. The use of spatial improvement ($c_1^{sp} \ne 0$) affects the form of the wavefunctions but does not spoil the positivity of the metric.
