\chapter{Research and Repair Roadmap: Towards a Rigorous Proof}
\label{app:roadmap_repair}

\section*{Executive Summary}
According to the assessment of the current status of the Yang-Mills problem (as of December 2025), the Clay Mathematics Institute still lists the problem as unsolved. Consequently, this appendix does not claim that the conjecture is already settled. Instead, it presents a \textbf{Research/Repair Roadmap} designed to resolve the remaining open issues and transform the arguments into a rigorous proof.

The roadmap focuses on three critical ``blocking lemmas'':
\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(i)] A \textbf{uniform-in-volume gap} (or mixing inequality) that holds \textbf{beyond strong coupling}.
    \item[(ii)] A \textbf{controlled continuum limit}.
    \item[(iii)] A proof of \textbf{no phase transition / no gap-closing} along the bridge between regimes.
\end{enumerate}

\section{First ``Fix'': Logical Consistency and Audit-Readiness}

To ensure the logical consistency required for a Clay-level submission, this roadmap adopts the following structural repairs.

\subsection{A. Theorems vs. Program}
The logical dependencies are structured as a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG). Each logical component is categorized as:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RIGOROUS (Proved here):} Complete proof provided in the manuscript.
    \item \textbf{RIGOROUS (External):} Proved elsewhere; exact theorem statement cited and hypotheses verified.
    \item \textbf{CONDITIONAL:} Depends on an unproved hypothesis; stated explicitly as an assumption.
    \item \textbf{HEURISTIC:} Physics argument; cannot be used as a backbone.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{B. Remove Placeholders and Ambiguity}
Any ``Section ??'' or ``Theorem ??'' placeholders are fatal. Every placeholder must be replaced with:
\begin{itemize}
    \item A concrete theorem number.
    \item A precise statement (quantifiers, constants, domains).
    \item A proof or a verified external citation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{C. Eliminate Circularity Explicitly}
To avoid the circularity trap (Mass Gap $\Rightarrow$ Exponential Decay $\Rightarrow$ Mixing/LSI $\Rightarrow$ Mass Gap), we establish the following strict logical order:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Primary Quantity:} Prove a uniform Log-Sobolev Inequality (LSI) or uniform spectral gap for the transfer matrix generator from first principles (e.g., via hierarchical block decomposition).
    \item \textbf{Derived Consequence:} Deduce correlation decay from the spectral gap.
\end{enumerate}
We explicitly avoid assuming correlation decay to prove the spectral gap in the critical intermediate regime.

\subsection{D. The Clay Target Statement}
The final target of this roadmap is the exact statement required by the Clay Millennium Problem:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Existence of a nontrivial Quantum Field Theory on $\mathbb{R}^4$ meeting strong axioms (OS/Wightman).
    \item A strictly positive mass gap: $\mathrm{Spec}(H) \subset \{0\} \cup [\Delta, \infty)$ with $\Delta > 0$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Identify the True ``Blocking Lemmas''}

The following ``blocking lemmas'' are the specific issues that must be turned into proved theorems:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Uniform-in-$L$ Gap/Bounds (Thermodynamic Limit):} $\lim_{L\to\infty} \Delta_L(\beta) = \Delta(\beta) > 0$ for all $\beta > 0$.
    \item \textbf{Continuum Limit ($a\to 0$) with Positive Physical Gap:} $\Delta_{\mathrm{phys}} = \lim_{a\to 0} a^{-1} \Delta(\beta(a)) > 0$.
    \item \textbf{No Phase Transition / No Gap Closure:} For all $\beta > 0$, no phase transition occurs and the gap does not close.
\end{enumerate}

\section{A Workable Roadmap: Lattice $\to$ Uniform IR Control $\to$ Continuum $\to$ Gap}

Each step below represents a proof obligation with a clear success criterion.

\subsection{Step 0: Definitions and Equivalences}
\textbf{Goal:} Choose one precise definition of ``mass gap'' and make every equivalence explicit.

Typical constructive route:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Euclidean OS axioms (reflection positivity, etc.) $\Rightarrow$ reconstruction of a Hilbert space and Hamiltonian (transfer matrix method).
    \item Mass gap $\Leftrightarrow$ exponential decay of suitable 2-point (or connected) correlators.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Deliverable:} A lemma stating exactly which correlators and which decay imply the spectral gap in the reconstructed Hamiltonian.

\subsection{Step 1: Lattice Construction + OS Reflection Positivity}
\textbf{Goal:} Define lattice Yang–Mills with Wilson action and show:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Partition function is well-defined.
    \item Reflection positivity holds.
    \item Transfer matrix exists and is positive/self-adjoint in finite volume.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Deliverable:} A clean theorem + proof (or precise citations) for OS reflection positivity and transfer matrix positivity.

\subsection{Step 2: Strong Coupling Anchor (Rigorous)}
\textbf{Goal:} Prove an infinite-volume mass gap for $\beta < \beta_0$ using cluster/polymer expansion.

\textbf{Deliverable:} Explicit constants:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\beta_0 > 0$.
    \item Correlation length $\xi(\beta) < \infty$ for $\beta < \beta_0$.
    \item Exponential decay bounds uniform in volume.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Step 3: Intermediate Coupling}
This is the core mathematical challenge. We outline two strategies.

\subsubsection{Strategy A (Direct): Uniform Mixing Inequality $\Rightarrow$ Uniform Spectral Gap}
\textbf{Goal:} Prove a uniform Log-Sobolev inequality (LSI) for the Gibbs measure on the lattice, with a constant independent of volume $L$.

\textbf{Requirements:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item A block decomposition scheme (hierarchical) that gives a recursion for LSI/Poincaré constants.
    \item A \textit{non-circular} ``conditional tensorization'' step that bounds boundary-induced correlations.
    \item Uniform control of boundary measures so constants don't degrade like $e^{cL^4}$.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Deliverable:} A theorem of the form:
\[
\Delta_L(\beta) \ge c(\beta) > 0 \quad \text{for all } L, \ \beta \in [\beta_0, \beta_1],
\]
with explicit $c(\beta)$ and no hidden ``assuming the gap'' inputs.

\subsubsection{Strategy B (Interpolation): Connect YM to a Theory with a Known Gap}
\textbf{Goal:} Connect YM to a theory with a known gap (e.g., Adjoint QCD / SYM).

\textbf{Requirements:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The endpoint theory is rigorously gapped} (typically $\mathcal{N}=1$ SYM).
    \item \textbf{Analyticity along the interpolation path} so no phase transition occurs.
    \item \textbf{Decoupling limit} (adjoint mass $m\to\infty$) preserves the gap and produces pure YM.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Deliverable:} A theorem of the form
\[
\inf_{m\in(0,\infty)}\Delta(m,\beta) > 0
\]
(or an explicit analytic continuation argument that implies it) plus a controlled decoupling theorem.

\subsection{Step 4: No Phase Transition / No Gap Closure}
\textbf{Goal:} Ensure the gap doesn't vanish as $\beta \to \infty$.

\textbf{Deliverable:} A statement like:
\begin{itemize}
    \item ``For all $\beta > 0$, the free energy is analytic,'' or
    \item ``For all $\beta > 0$, $\Delta(\beta) > 0$ and hence no critical point exists.''
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Step 5: Continuum Limit Construction (UV Control)}
\textbf{Goal:} Constructive continuum limit with axiom control (e.g., Balaban-type RG).

\textbf{Deliverable:} A theorem of the form:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Schwinger functions converge (after renormalization) as $a\to 0$.
    \item OS axioms hold in the limit.
    \item Reconstructed Wightman QFT exists.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Step 6: Bridge ``Lattice Gap'' to ``Physical Gap''}
\textbf{Goal:} Ensure $\Delta_{\text{phys}} = \lim a^{-1} \Delta_{\text{lat}}(\beta(a)) > 0$.

\textbf{Deliverable:} A rigorous inequality of the schematic form
\[
\Delta(\beta) \ge c \sqrt{\sigma(\beta)}
\]
plus a proof that $\sigma_{\text{phys}} > 0$ in the continuum limit.

\section{Work Breakdown Structure}

\subsection{WP1 — Intermediate Coupling Control (IR)}
\textbf{Acceptance Test:} A published-quality proof of uniform-in-$L$ mixing/gap for all $\beta > 0$ (or at least $\beta \in [\beta_0, \infty)$), with constants and no circularity.

\subsection{WP2 — No Phase Transitions / Analyticity (Global Control)}
\textbf{Acceptance Test:} Proof that the relevant thermodynamic functions are analytic (or that the gap cannot close) along the required parameter path.

\subsection{WP3 — Continuum Limit (UV)}
\textbf{Acceptance Test:} A constructive continuum limit theorem with OS/Wightman axioms verified.

\section{Strategic Choice}
To produce a convincing roadmap, we commit to one primary nonperturbative mechanism:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Primary Path:} Uniform LSI/gap via hierarchical methods.
    \item \textbf{Alternative Path:} Adjoint interpolation with rigorous SYM endpoint.
\end{itemize}
The logical spine of the proof will follow the chosen path to ensure audit-readiness.

\section{Conclusion}
This roadmap replaces the claim of a ``complete proof'' with a structured, verifiable program. By focusing on the three blockers—uniform gap, continuum limit, and no phase transition—and adhering to the strict logical dependencies outlined above, this program aims to provide a rigorous resolution to the Yang-Mills Existence and Mass Gap problem.
