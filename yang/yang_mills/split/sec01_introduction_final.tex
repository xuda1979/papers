\subsection{Structure of the Proof (The RG-Scale Perspective)}

We restructure the classical "three coupling regimes" argument into a coherent \textbf{Scale-Dependent Renormalization Group} flow, following the physical process of integrating out degrees of freedom from the ultraviolet (UV) to the infrared (IR).

\subsubsection*{Stage I: The Ultraviolet (Asymptotic Freedom)}
At fine scales ($a \to 0, \beta \to \infty$), the effective coupling is small. We rely on the rigorous \textbf{Perturbative Renormalization Group} developed by Balaban. This stage controls the regularity of the theory and establishes the existence of the continuum limit trajectory.

\subsubsection*{Stage II: The Crossover (Computer-Assisted Bridge)}
As the RG integrates out fluctuations, the effective coupling grows. At intermediate scales, the flow leaves the domain of perturbation theory but has not yet reached strong coupling. 
We employ a \textbf{Computer-Assisted Proof (CAP)} to verify:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The flow remains contracting in a "Tube" of effective actions (Tube Contraction).
    \item No non-trivial fixed points (phase transitions) interrupt the flow towards the disordered phase.
\end{enumerate}
This is validated using rigorous interval arithmetic over the continuous parameter range $[\VerBetaIntermediateMin, \VerBetaIntermediateMax]$, bridging the perturbative and non-perturbative regimes.

\subsubsection*{Stage III: The Infrared (Confinement)}
Once the effective coupling enters the strong coupling domain (Scale $k_*$), we apply the \textbf{Analytic Cluster Expansion} for the effective action.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Decay of Correlations:} We prove exponential decay of Wilson loops at the coarse scale.
    \item \textbf{Mass Gap:} We demonstrate that this decay at scale $k_*$ implies a strictly positive spectrum for the transfer matrix of the continuum theory.
\end{enumerate}
