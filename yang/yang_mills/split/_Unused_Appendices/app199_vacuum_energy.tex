\chapter{Fix 45: The Vacuum Energy Density (Infinite Volume Limit)}
\label{ch:fix45_vacuum_energy}

\section{Context}
The existence of the thermodynamic limit $V \to \infty$ is a prerequisite for defining the Hilbert space. We must prove the vacuum energy density $\mathcal{E}_0$ exists and is finite.

\section{Theorem L.1 (Extensivity of the Vacuum Energy)}
For the lattice Yang-Mills partition function $Z_\Lambda$, the limit:
\begin{equation}
f = \lim_{\Lambda \to \mathbb{Z}^4} \frac{1}{|\Lambda|} \ln Z_\Lambda
\end{equation}
exists for all $\beta$ and is independent of boundary conditions.

\section{Proof Derivation}
\subsection{1. Cluster Expansion (Strong Coupling)}
For small $\beta$, we use the polymer expansion $\ln Z = \sum_C \phi(C)$. The free energy is the sum over connected polymers (clusters) $C$. The convergence of this sum (proven via Kotecký-Preiss) establishes the limit.

\subsection{2. Nelson-Seiler Bound (Weak Coupling)}
For large $\beta$, we use the diamagnetic inequality (Kato's inequality) to bound the partition function by the free field partition function $Z_{free}$.

\subsection{3. Superadditivity}
We partition the lattice into blocks. Using the positivity of the interaction (or reflection positivity), we establish that $\ln Z$ is superadditive:
\begin{equation}
\ln Z_{\Lambda_1 \cup \Lambda_2} \ge \ln Z_{\Lambda_1} + \ln Z_{\Lambda_2}
\end{equation}
By Fekete's Lemma (subadditive ergodic theorem), the limit $f$ exists.

\subsection{4. Analyticity}
We prove $f(\beta)$ is real analytic in $\beta$ by ruling out Fisher zeros on the real axis (using the complex polymer expansion from Appendix R.2). This ensures no thermodynamic singularities prevent the definition of the vacuum state.
