This manuscript develops a rigorous framework toward the constructive construction of four-dimensional Euclidean Yang--Mills theory and the proof of a positive mass gap for compact simple gauge groups (with emphasis on $SU(2)$ and $SU(3)$). We provide detailed, verifiable lattice foundations (reflection positivity, transfer matrix formalism, and finite-volume spectral considerations) and explicit strong-coupling estimates via convergent expansions.

The remaining steps required for a complete resolution---uniform control through intermediate coupling, non-perturbative scale-setting at weak coupling, and a fully rigorous continuum limit with a nonzero mass gap---are formulated precisely as a program. Proposed routes (e.g. log-Sobolev/functional-inequality approaches, renormalization-group strategies, and possible interpolation ideas) are stated with explicit hypotheses and are not asserted as unconditional theorems unless proved in full detail.

Accordingly, any phenomenological relations used in the physics literature (for example bounds of the schematic form $\Delta \gtrsim \sqrt{\sigma}$) are treated as motivation and are not promoted to first-principles theorems without a complete proof.
