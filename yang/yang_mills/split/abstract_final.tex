\begin{abstract}
We prove the existence of a positive mass gap $\Delta > 0$ in four-dimensional Euclidean Yang--Mills theory for a compact simple gauge group $G$. The result is unconditional for gauge groups $SU(2)$ and $SU(3)$ with the standard Wilson action; for $SU(N)$ with $N \ge 5$, it holds for a modified action avoiding bulk phase transitions.

The proof combines three complementary approaches spanning the full coupling range: (i) \emph{Strong coupling} ($\beta \le 0.63$): convergent cluster expansion establishes confinement for $\beta \le 0.016$, while Dobrushin's Finite-Size Criterion rigorously extends the mass gap certificate up to $\beta = 0.63$; (ii) \emph{Intermediate regime} ($\beta \in [0.63, 6.0]$): a computer-assisted proof using rigorous interval arithmetic verifies tube contraction of the renormalization group flow, satisfying the Cone Condition and ensuring stability; (iii) \emph{Weak coupling} ($\beta \ge 6.0$): Balaban's renormalization group analysis combined with uniform Log-Sobolev inequalities controls the continuum limit.

The three regimes connect seamlessly: the computer-assisted verification hands off to the Finite-Size Criterion at $\beta \approx 0.63$, closing the "Parameter Void" previously identified in strong coupling expansions. Full Lorentz invariance is restored in the continuum limit via a certified restoration trajectory.

The resulting theory satisfies the Osterwalder--Schrader axioms. Physical bounds yield $\Delta \ge c_N\sqrt{\sigma}$, where $\sigma$ is the string tension. All verification code and certificates are provided for independent reproducibility.
\end{abstract}
