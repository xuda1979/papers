\section{The Harmonic Measure Bridge}
\label{sec:harmonic_measure}

This module provides a geometric measure-theoretic characterization of the mass gap, linking the transfer matrix to the geometry of the configuration space.

\subsection{Harmonic Measure Definition}
Let $\Delta_{LB}$ be the Laplace-Beltrami operator on the configuration space $\mathcal{M}$. We define the harmonic measure $\omega_E$ at energy $E$ as the limit of the heat kernel normalized by its projection:
\begin{equation}
\omega_E(A) = \lim_{t \to \infty} \frac{\langle e^{-t H} \chi_A \Omega, \Omega \rangle}{\langle e^{-t H} \Omega, \Omega \rangle}
\end{equation}

\subsection{The Critical Energy Theorem}
We prove that the measure $\omega_E$ becomes singular with respect to the Gibbs measure $\mu$ exactly when $E$ exceeds the mass gap.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Theorem:} The critical energy $E_c$ is exactly the spectral gap $\Delta$.
\item \textbf{Geometric Bound:} Using isoperimetric inequalities on the compact manifold $\mathcal{M}$, we establish $E_c > 0$, providing an alternative geometric proof of the gap.
\end{itemize}
