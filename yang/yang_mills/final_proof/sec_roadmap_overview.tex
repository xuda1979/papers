\subsection{The Rigorous Proof Chain}
The proof proceeds via the following 4-step chain, which avoids circularity and unnecessary dependencies:

\begin{description}
    \item[Step 1: Hypothesis RG-Stability(a)]
    \textit{Assumption:} There exists a renormalized trajectory such that the effective actions satisfy uniform Hessian lower bounds on gauge-fixed physical directions, exponential decay of mixed second derivatives, and large-field suppression.
    
    \item[Step 2: Hypothesis Tightness (UV Regularity)]
    \textit{Assumption:} The family of lattice measures is tight in a suitable negative Sobolev space $H^{-s}$ (for $s>2$) or Besov space, ensuring the existence of a continuum limit measure $\mu$.

    \item[Step 3: Theorem: OS Reconstruction and Clustering]
    \textit{Implication:} If RG-Stability holds, the limit measure $\mu$ satisfies the Osterwalder-Schrader axioms and exhibits exponential clustering of correlations.
    
    \item[Step 4: Theorem: Mass Gap]
    \textit{Implication:} Exponential clustering of the unique vacuum state implies a strictly positive mass gap in the energy-momentum spectrum of the associated Hamiltonian.
\end{description}

This structure replaces the previous "Five Theorems" approach and decouples the mass gap from the confinement problem.
