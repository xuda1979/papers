\section{rigorous QGW foliation}
\label{app:qgw_foliation}

This appendix is a second rigorous side program. It is not part of the
repository's audited proof spine and it does not discharge the Hamiltonian
identification theorem boundary.

The intended role of a Quantum Gromov--Wasserstein foliation theory would be to
supply a rigidity mechanism for the finite packet realization problems described
in Appendix~\ref{sec:hamiltonian-identification-boundary}. Concretely, one would
need theorem-level results of the following kind:
\begin{enumerate}
\item packetwise reconstruction estimates on the Wilson-loop packets $W_N$ and
rational-time packets $F_N$;
\item a rigidity theorem showing that packetwise agreement under the common OS
constraints forces equality of the continuum state and equality of the
Euclidean action;
\item an upgrade theorem from packet equality to the full Wilson-loop state and
the full Euclidean semigroup.
\end{enumerate}

No such theorems are proved here. In particular, the present appendix does
\emph{not} prove tuple-fiber constancy, does \emph{not} prove generator-level
injectivity for the Yang--Mills packet dictionary, and does \emph{not} prove the
blocked action/state extension steps. Those remain open obligations in the audit
artifacts and in the roadmap.

Accordingly, this appendix should be cited only as a conjectural research
framework. Any statement that it ``resolves'' Blocker~3 or closes the mass-gap
proof is false at the current revision.
