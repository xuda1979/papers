\section{Rigorous mass-gap synthesis: corrections and new lower bounds}
\label{app:rigorous_mass_gap_synthesis}

This appendix makes four rigorous contributions absent from all previous appendices.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A complete proof of volume-monotonicity under open boundary conditions,
  supplying the tensor-product factorization omitted in \S\ref{ssec:vol_monotone}
  (\S\ref{ssec:vol_mono_corrected}).
\item A proof that $\beta\mapsto m(\Lambda,\beta)$ is non-increasing via
  log-convexity of the second transfer-matrix eigenvalue (\S\ref{ssec:coupling_mono}).
\item An infinite-volume mass gap proved by a new method---the
  Dobrushin--Poincar\'{e} criterion---giving an explicit volume-independent
  lower bound $m_\infty(\beta)\ge\log(1/(15N\beta))$ for $\beta<1/(15N)$,
  independent of the cluster expansion (\S\ref{ssec:dobrushin_poincare}).
\item A precise minimal reformulation of the Clay conjecture as two
  explicit statements (A) and (B), with a rigorous proof that
  (A)$+$(B)$\Rightarrow$(Clay) (\S\ref{ssec:residual_gap}).
\end{enumerate}

Throughout G=SU(N), N>=2, and beta>0.
