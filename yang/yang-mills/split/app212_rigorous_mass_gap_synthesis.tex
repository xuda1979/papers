
\section{Rigorous mass-gap synthesis: corrections and new lower bounds}
\label{app:rigorous_mass_gap_synthesis}

This appendix makes four rigorous contributions absent from all previous appendices.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A complete proof of volume-monotonicity under open boundary conditions,
  supplying the tensor-product factorization omitted in the original proof
  of Theorem~\ref{thm:volume_monotone}.
\item A proof that $\beta\mapsto m(\Lambda,\beta)$ is non-increasing in $\beta$
  via log-convexity of the second transfer-matrix eigenvalue.
\item An infinite-volume mass gap by a new method---the Dobrushin--Poincar\'{e} criterion---
  with explicit bound $m_\infty(\beta)\ge\log(1/(15N\beta))$ for $\beta<1/(15N)$,
  independent of the cluster expansion.
\item A precise minimal reformulation: (A)+(B) implies the Clay conjecture.
\end{enumerate}

Throughout $G=\mathrm{SU}(N)$, $N\ge 2$, $\beta>0$.
