\section{rigorous HST program}
\label{app:hst_rigorous}

This appendix records a conjectural research direction. It is not part of the
repository's audited proof spine and it does not resolve the theorem boundary
identified in Appendix~\ref{sec:hamiltonian-identification-boundary}.

The earlier version of this file claimed to ``rigorously discharge'' several
Yang--Mills blockers by introducing Holographic Stochastic Transport (HST) and a
``Quantum Weak KAM'' mechanism. Those claims were unsupported: no precise
Yang--Mills construction, no verified curvature-dimension estimate, no proved
continuum OS tightness theorem, no justified Trotter--Kato identification, and
no proved clustering-to-gap implication were supplied in the manuscript.
Accordingly, those claims are retracted here.

\subsection{What an HST program would need to prove}
For HST to become part of a rigorous mass-gap proof, one would need theorem-level
results of the following kind:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a well-posed gauge-covariant stochastic flow on a Yang--Mills configuration
space together with a proved invariant or asymptotically invariant continuum
measure;
\item a non-circular coercive inequality, such as an LSI or spectral-gap bound,
with constants uniform in the relevant approximating volumes and cutoffs;
\item a proof that reflection positivity and the other required
Osterwalder--Schrader axioms survive the continuum limit produced by that flow;
\item an identification theorem showing that the limiting semigroup generator and
its domain coincide with the OS-reconstructed Hamiltonian in the same
Yang--Mills model;
\item a separate argument transferring any such verified semigroup or clustering
estimate into a positive lower bound for the physical continuum Hamiltonian.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Why the current file does not close those steps}
At the current revision, none of the required results above is proved here.
In particular, this appendix does not provide:
\begin{itemize}
\item a mathematically defined HST dynamics on the non-abelian continuum theory;
\item a proved Bakry--\'Emery curvature lower bound in the Yang--Mills setting;
\item a non-circular derivation of a volume-uniform LSI constant;
\item a rigorous construction of the continuum OS measure by the proposed
transport map;
\item a proved resolvent-convergence theorem identifying the continuum generator
with the OS Hamiltonian;
\item a valid deduction of vacuum uniqueness or a positive mass gap from the
proposed framework.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Correct status}
HST should therefore be cited only as a rigorous side program. It may suggest
possible intermediate lemmas or model problems, but it does not currently prove
any of the four open obligations listed in
Appendix~\ref{sec:hamiltonian-identification-boundary}, and it does not convert
the repository into a proof of the four-dimensional $SU(3)$ Yang--Mills
mass-gap conjecture.
