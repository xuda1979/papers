\section{Final synthesis: audited status of the mass-gap program}
\label{app:mass_gap_synthesis}

The current repository does not prove the four-dimensional $SU(3)$ Yang--Mills
mass-gap conjecture. What it does provide is an audited theorem-boundary
organization of the existing evidence and of the remaining Yang--Mills-specific
obstructions.

\subsection{What is established in the audited spine}
The verification artifacts record the following items as present at the current
revision:
\begin{itemize}
\item approximant gap / decay input for the semigroup bridge;
\item semigroup-shaped operator convergence evidence;
\item continuum Schwinger-limit and OS-reconstruction evidence artifacts;
\item abstract projector/algebra/generator identification scaffolding.
\end{itemize}
These ingredients support only a theorem-boundary audit, not a completed proof.

\subsection{What remains open}
Appendix~\ref{sec:hamiltonian-identification-boundary} isolates the missing
Yang--Mills-specific bridge into four live theorem obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item convergence-side realization by the same continuum Schwinger family on
finite Wilson-loop packets;
\item convergence-side realization by the same Euclidean time-translation
action on finite rational-time packets;
\item extension of packetwise state agreement to equality of the full
continuum Wilson-loop state;
\item extension of packetwise action agreement to equality of the full
Euclidean semigroup.
\end{enumerate}
Until those four items are proved in the same Yang--Mills setting, no argument
in this manuscript yields a rigorous positive spectral gap for the
OS-reconstructed continuum Hamiltonian.

\subsection{Status conclusion}
The honest conclusion of the present manuscript is therefore:
\begin{quote}
The repository organizes constructive evidence, abstract bridge lemmas, and
explicit open theorem obligations, but it does not currently establish a
Clay-level proof of the $4D$ $SU(3)$ Yang--Mills existence and mass-gap
conjecture.
\end{quote}
