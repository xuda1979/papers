Please consider my manuscript entitled

"Critical Dirichlet Spectral Zeta Asymptotics on Graphs of Quadratic Volume Growth"

for publication as a Research Article in Frontiers of Mathematics in China.

The paper studies the Dirichlet spectral zeta value on large finite domains in infinite graphs with quadratic volume growth. Its main result gives a criterion for the critical asymptotic Z_H(1) ~ |H| log |H| in terms of geometric regularity, on-diagonal heat-kernel asymptotics, and a long-time Dirichlet estimate. For metric-ball exhaustions we prove the ratio limit with the heat-kernel constant, obtain a quantitative O(|H|) refinement under stronger on-diagonal control, and derive several mesoscopic spectral consequences. We also formulate corresponding results for abstract exhaustions and verify the required long-time and mesoscopic inputs on square domains for an explicit axial-diagonal family.

We believe the manuscript fits Frontiers of Mathematics in China because it lies at the intersection of probability theory, graph theory, and analysis, and because it develops a general result together with explicit model verification in a setting of current interest.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. I have approved the submission and have no conflict of interest to declare.
