Please consider my manuscript entitled

"Critical Dirichlet Spectral Zeta Asymptotics on Graphs of Quadratic Volume Growth"

for publication in Potential Analysis as an Original Paper.

The paper studies the Dirichlet spectral zeta value on large finite domains in infinite graphs with quadratic volume growth. Its main result gives a criterion for the critical asymptotic Z_H(1) ~ |H| log |H| in terms of geometric regularity, on-diagonal heat-kernel asymptotics, and a long-time Dirichlet estimate. For metric-ball exhaustions it proves the ratio limit with the heat-kernel constant, obtains a quantitative O(|H|) refinement under stronger on-diagonal control, and derives mesoscopic spectral consequences. It also formulates corresponding results for abstract exhaustions and verifies the required long-time and mesoscopic inputs on square domains for an explicit axial-diagonal family.

I believe the manuscript is a strong fit for Potential Analysis because it lies at the intersection of potential theory, probability on graphs, geometric analysis, and functional-analytic aspects of Dirichlet Laplacians. The paper combines heat-kernel methods, Green-function asymptotics, and spectral consequences in a setting that connects discrete potential theory with probabilistic and analytic techniques.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. The author has no competing interests.
