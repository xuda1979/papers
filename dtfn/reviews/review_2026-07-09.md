# Critical Self-Review of "Discrete Token-Flow Networks" (DTFN)

**Reviewer mode**: Adversarial NeurIPS/ICML-style review
**Date**: 2026-07-09

## Summary
The paper proposes Discrete Token-Flow Networks (DTFNs), a sequence
model whose forward pass is a conservative finite-volume scheme for a
hyperbolic conservation law on a 1D token grid. The paper claims exact
mass conservation, pointwise non-negativity, a discrete maximum
principle, and demonstrates on synthetic tasks (Dyck-1/2, copy) that
DTFNs extrapolate exactly to 5× training length.

## Strengths
1. **Novel inductive bias**: framing language modeling as a hyperbolic
   conservation law is genuinely new. The connection between numerical
   analysis and sequence modeling is underexplored.
2. **Provable guarantees**: exact conservation, non-negativity, max
   principle, CFL-stability — all proven rigorously.
3. **Clean experimental story on Dyck**: 100% accuracy at 5× training
   length is a strong extrapolation result.
4. **Universality theorem** (Thm 4) gives expressive-power guarantee.

## Major Weaknesses (W#)

### W1: Only synthetic tasks — no real-language evaluation
The paper itself flags this as a limitation. A NeurIPS reviewer would
reject on this alone. We have `dtfn_real_lang.py` (Tiny Shakespeare)
referenced in the `__init__.py` but the file doesn't exist and no
results are reported.

### W2: Innovation modules implemented but not in the paper
The `code/innovations/` directory contains 5 major extensions:
  - WENO5 (5th-order accurate) — exp2 confirms order 4.76 (≈5)
  - CPSA (conservation-preserving sparse attention)
  - 2D-DTFN (dimensional splitting)
  - Adaptive-DTFN (per-position CFL)
  - SSM bridge (linearised DTFN ↔ SSM)

The paper's "Limitations and future work" section LITERALLY lists
these as future work, but they are ALREADY IMPLEMENTED with results
sitting in `code/results/innovations_v3.json`. This is a major
oversight — the paper is underselling itself.

### W3: WENO5 convergence order drops at high L due to float32
The WENO5 order is 4.76 for L=32→64 but drops to negative at L=256→512
because the error hits float32 precision (~1e-6). Fix: use float64
for the convergence test, OR report only the first 2-3 refinement
levels where the order is cleanly 5.

### W4: SSM bridge is asserted, not verified
`exp4_ssm_correspondence` shows predicted spectral radius 0.79-0.91
but actual is 1.0000 — the linearization doesn't match the nonlinear
update. The "jacobian_max_error" is 0.16-0.26 which is large. We
should either (a) restrict to small perturbations, or (b) be honest
about the discrepancy.

### W5: No baseline comparison on the new innovations
We have DTFN-Adaptive achieving 100% on Dyck-1, but no comparison to
DTFN-U/B/MUSCL on the same seed/config. Is the improvement due to
the innovation or just seed variance?

### W6: CPSA conservation error is 3e-5, not machine precision
This contradicts the paper's central claim of EXACT conservation.
We need to either (a) fix CPSA to be exactly conservative, or (b)
be explicit that the attention extension is approximately
conservative (with a tunable tolerance).

### W7: Theorem 4 (universality) is informal
"receptive field K" claim is vague — for a Kružkov entropy solution
you need Lipschitz regularity and a monotone scheme; the theorem
should state these hypotheses precisely.

### W8: No ablation on FluxNet width/depth
The FluxNet is a 2-layer MLP with 32 hidden units. No sensitivity
analysis. A reviewer would ask: does width matter? Does depth?

### W9: Dyck-2 results (61% accuracy) are weak
DTFN-FULL achieves only 61% on Dyck-2 — this is barely above
majority baseline (50%). Need to either (a) show the innovations
(WENO5, Adaptive, CPSA) close this gap, or (b) be upfront that
DTFNs struggle on multi-bracket counting.

### W10: No comparison to Mamba/S4/state-space models
Given the SSM bridge contribution, we should compare to at least
one SSM baseline on Dyck-1 length extrapolation.

### W11: Wall-clock scaling only goes to 4096 tokens
Modern long-context benchmarks are 32k+. The scaling story should
extend to 16k or 32k to be competitive.

### W12: No statistical significance / multiple seeds
Most tables report a single number. Need mean ± std over ≥3 seeds.

## Minor Issues (M#)

- M1: Abstract says "5× length gap" but Table 5 shows 100% to L=100
  from L_train=20 — that's 5×, OK, but L=100 is still tiny.
- M2: Bibliography missing key references: Mamba (Gu & Dao 2023),
  S5 (Smith et al. 2023), RWKV (Peng et al. 2023).
- M3: Figure 1 (architecture) is a PNG, not a PDF — should be
  vector graphics.
- M4: Section 5.8 (adversarial stability) shows DTFN-FULL has
  conservation error 0.13 under perturbation — this is large and
  should be explained more carefully.
- M5: The proof of Thm 2 (max principle) in the appendix relies on
  the source term being bounded by `σ * max|S_i|`; this should be
  stated as an explicit assumption, not buried in the proof.

## Recommended Actions (Priority Order)

1. **[CRITICAL] Add innovations section to the paper** — WENO5, CPSA,
   2D, Adaptive, SSM bridge are all implemented; report their results.
2. **[CRITICAL] Add real-language evaluation** — Tiny Shakespeare,
   even at character level.
3. **[HIGH] Re-run WENO5 convergence in float64** to show clean
   order-5 convergence to L=512.
4. **[HIGH] Run multi-seed experiments** on the innovation variants
   vs baseline DTFN-U.
5. **[HIGH] Run on Huanxin ASI3 NPU** to demonstrate the model
   scales beyond M4 CPU.
6. **[MEDIUM] Fix or qualify the SSM bridge** — either restrict to
   small perturbations or be explicit about the discrepancy.
7. **[MEDIUM] Add SSM baseline (S4 or Mamba)** to Dyck-1 extrapolation.
8. **[MEDIUM] Fix CPSA conservation** to be exact, or document the
   tolerance explicitly.
9. **[LOWER] Extend wall-clock scaling to 16k+** tokens.
