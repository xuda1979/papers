## Issues found and fixed in this session

### R1: Off-by-one in variant count (abstract + intro + method)
The abstract said "seven instantiations", the intro said "seven
variants", and the Method section's variant list had 7 entries, but
all three locations then enumerated 8 variants (U, B, MUSCL, FULL,
WENO5, Adaptive, CPSA, FCT). DTFN--FCT was defined in §4.7 but omitted
from the Method "DTFN variants" list. Fixed:
  - Abstract: "seven" → "eight instantiations".
  - Intro contribution bullet: "seven" → "eight variants".
  - Method §3.2: "seven" → "eight variants" and added DTFN--FCT to
    the itemised list with a one-line description.

### R2: Conservation-table caption contradicted the data
Table 4 (conservation) reported DTFN--FCT conservation error as
$1.21\times 10^{-4}$, but the caption and body text claimed FCT was
"conservative to float32 round-off ($\sim 10^{-8}$)" alongside U, B,
MUSCL, WENO5. The $10^{-4}$ figure is real and comes from the
learned-limiter sigmoid $\phi = \sigma(\cdot)$ being rounded in
float32 (the telescoping flux is exact in exact arithmetic). Fixed:
  - Body text (§6.1): separated FCT from the flux-only variants and
    added a paragraph explaining the sigmoid-precision residual.
  - Table 4 caption: now says "FCT, CPSA, and Adaptive are
    conservative to $\sim 10^{-4}$--$10^{-5}$".
  - Figure 3 caption: updated to mention the two precision tiers.
  - §6.11 (FCT conservation paragraph): corrected the mislabeling of
    $1.2\times 10^{-4}$ as "float32 round-off" — it is float32
    sigmoid precision.

### R3: Tiny Shakespeare DTFN--B perplexity was misleading
DTFN--B achieved perplexity 1.04 on Tiny Shakespeare, but DTFN--B is
bidirectional/non-causal: the backward stack propagates information
from future tokens to past positions, so as a next-token predictor
the model sees the right context it is trying to predict. A
perplexity of 1.04 therefore reflects a masked-token (cloze) task,
not a causal-LM task, and is not comparable to causal-LM perplexities
in the literature. Fixed:
  - Rewrote §6.14 body to explicitly flag DTFN--B as non-causal and
    reinterpret the 1.04 as a cloze result, not a causal-LM result.
  - Updated Table 12 caption to state the causal/non-causal
    distinction.
  - Reframed DTFN--U (perplexity 11.12) as the meaningful causal-LM
    data point.

### R4: SSM-bridge table said "top eigenvalue is exactly 1.0"
The SSM-bridge table (Table 10) reports actual top eigenvalues of

  - Caption: now says "predicted top eigenvalue is 1.0; actual is
    1.0004–1.0020, confirming conservation to finite-difference
    precision".
  - Body text: rewritten to report the actual range and attribute
    the deviation to $\mathcal{O}(\delta^2)$ finite-difference error.

## Second pass (2026-07-13, later) — fresh adversarial review

A second adversarial review pass found and fixed six additional
issues:

### R1: K-ablation caption contradicted the data
The caption of Table~\ref{tab:ablation-K} claimed
"$K\in\{2,3,5\}$ is the sweet spot", but the table data shows K=1
has the best loss (0.0043) and tied-best accuracy (0.9998), while
$K\in\{2,3,5\}$ are slightly *worse* (0.9986). The body text also
claimed "Beyond $K=5$ the loss slightly increases due to
overfitting", but K=12 recovers to 0.9998 accuracy. Rewrote both
the caption and the body to accurately describe the data: the loss
is minimised at the extremes, performance is flat across the middle
values, and $K=3$ is adopted as a robust default.

### R2: Dyck-2 "five seeds" overclaim
The abstract, intro, and discussion all claimed "100% accuracy on
Dyck-1 and Dyck-2 across five seeds", but the Dyck-2 table caption
explicitly says "single seed, 8 epochs". Fixed all three locations
to say "Dyck-1 across five random seeds and on Dyck-2 (single
seed)". Also added a sentence to the Dyck-2 section acknowledging
that a five-seed Dyck-2 study is a natural follow-up.

### R3: SSM bridge contraction bound too tight
The caption and body said the contraction rate matches "to within
$0.08$", but the max observed gap is $0.0829$ (at
$d{=}4,L{=}8,\lambda{=}0.5$), which exceeds $0.08$. Updated to
"to within $0.09$" and cited the worst-case configuration
explicitly.

### R4: WENO5 "float64 round-off floor" mischaracterisation
The caption and body attributed the WENO5 order degradation
($4.58 \to 3.05$) to "hitting the float64 round-off floor at
$L=1024$". But the error at $L=1024$ is $3.16\times 10^{-9}$,
roughly four orders of magnitude above the float64 round-off floor
for $O(1)$ solutions ($\sim 10^{-13}$). The order decline is
better explained by the spatial error approaching the
temporal-discretisation floor set by the fixed CFL=0.3 Euler step.
Rewrote both the caption and body accordingly.

### R6: CPSA conservation argument incomplete
The CPSA conservation claim
"$\sum_i \tilde p_i = \sum_j p_j$ when the windows tile the
sequence" elided the boundary effect: near the sequence boundaries
the windows are truncated, so the doubly-stochastic property does
not hold exactly. Added a caveat that conservation holds exactly
only in the interior, and that with the boundary-mask
renormalisation the residual is $O(w\|p\|_\infty/L)$, vanishing as
$L \gg w$.

### R7: L1-contraction proof gap (Step 1--2)
The proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:l1} claimed the perturbation
$\delta^k = p^k - \tilde p^k$ obeys the "same" convex-combination
coefficients $\alpha^{(k)}_{i,s}$ as the state, with the
coefficients depending on $p^k$ but not on $\tilde p^k$. This is
not generally true for a neural FluxNet with state-dependent
coefficients, and the claim that the coefficients are "the same"
for $p$ and $\tilde p$ was unjustified. Replaced Steps 1--2 with
the correct Crandall--Majda monotone-scheme L1-contraction lemma,
which gives the contraction *without* requiring state-independent
coefficients — the key step tests the monotone update against
$\operatorname{sign}(p^k - \tilde p^k)$ and uses the telescoping of
the conservative flux. This is the right argument for the neural
setting. Step 3 ($\ell_1 \to \ell_\infty$) is unchanged.

## Third pass (2026-07-13, third review) — additional consistency fixes

A third adversarial review pass found and fixed eleven more issues,
mostly overclaims and cross-reference inconsistencies:

### R8: Conservation theorem omitted WENO5 and FCT
Theorem~\ref{thm:conservation} stated "This holds for DTFN--U,
DTFN--B, and DTFN--MUSCL", omitting DTFN--WENO5 (which also conserves
exactly as a flux-only scheme) and DTFN--FCT (which conserves exactly
in exact arithmetic per Theorem~\ref{thm:fct}). Expanded the theorem
statement to enumerate all eight variants and characterise each one's
conservation residual.

### R9: SSM bridge "to within $10^{-3}$" too tight
The intro claimed the SSM bridge spectral structure is "verified
empirically to within $10^{-3}$", but the top-eigenvalue deviation
is up to $2\times 10^{-3}$ and the contraction gap is up to $0.083$.
Updated to "top eigenvalue matches to within $2\times 10^{-3}$ and
the contraction rate to within $0.09$".

### R10: Discussion overclaimed "exact" conservation for all variants
The discussion said "DTFNs satisfy *exact* mass conservation ...
by construction" without qualifying that DTFN--FULL (non-conservative
source), DTFN--Adaptive (non-constant $\lambda_i$), and DTFN--CPSA/FCT
(float32 round-off) do not satisfy exact conservation. Rewrote to
distinguish the flux-only variants (exact) from the remaining
variants (controlled, characterised residuals).

### R11: Abstract overclaimed "all satisfy the three invariants exactly"
Same issue as R10, in the abstract. Rewrote to "flux-only variants
satisfy the three invariants exactly, while the remaining variants
satisfy them up to controlled, characterised residuals".

### R12: Intro overclaimed "all variants satisfy *exact* mass conservation"
Same issue as R10/R11, in the contributions list. Rewrote
consistently with R11. Also reordered the theorem references from
the confusing "Theorems 4--6, 1--2, 3" to the sequential
"Theorems 1--3 and 4--6".

### R13: Method section overclaimed "prove them for all DTFN variants"
The invariants subsection intro said "state the three structural
invariants and prove them for all DTFN variants", which is
inconsistent with the per-variant residuals. Rewrote to distinguish
the flux-only variants (exact) from the remaining variants
(controlled residuals).

### R14: NPU scaling table non-monotonicity unexplained
The DTFN--U row of Table~\ref{tab:npu-scaling} is non-monotonic at
small $L$ (4.1 ms at $L=256$ vs 3.4 ms at $L=512$). Added a sentence
explaining that at small $L$ the kernel-launch and Python-dispatch
overhead dominates the $\mathcal{O}(L)$ compute, and the linear
trend is clearly visible from $L \ge 1024$ onward. (The appendix
Table~\ref{tab:cpu-scaling} already had a similar note.)

### R15: FCT "verify exact conservation" contradicted the measured error
The FCT conservation paragraph said "We verify exact conservation and
positivity of FCT--DTFN", but the very next sentence reported a
$\le 1.2\times 10^{-4}$ conservation error. Removed the word
"exact".

### R16: Stability table "majority within $[0.95, 1.05]$" was 50%, not a majority
The body text said "the majority of values within $[0.95, 1.05]$",
but counting the values in Table~\ref{tab:stability} gives exactly
10/20 = 50% in that range, which is not a majority. Rewrote to
"about half of the values within $[0.95, 1.05]$".

### R17: Adaptive CFL body text contradicted its theorem
The adaptive CFL subsection (Section~\ref{sec:adaptive}) said "the
scheme inherits conservation (the $\lambda_i$ weight the telescoping
flux)", but Theorem~\ref{thm:adaptive} explicitly says the scheme
does NOT conserve exactly when $\lambda_i$ varies — the residual is
$\sum_i (\lambda_{i+1}-\lambda_i) F_{i+1/2}$. Rewrote the body text
to match the theorem.

### R18: Conservation table caption misattributed Adaptive's error source
The caption attributed DTFN--Adaptive's $\sim 10^{-5}$ conservation
error to "learned-limiter sigmoid / softmax computations in float32",
but DTFN--Adaptive has no sigmoid/softmax limiter — its
non-conservation comes from the per-position $\lambda_i$ variation
(Theorem~\ref{thm:adaptive}). Rewrote the caption and the body text
to correctly attribute the error: in the random-parameter regime the
$\lambda_i$ are close to constant at initialisation, so the
Theorem~\ref{thm:adaptive} residual is small and dominated by
float32 round-off; after training the $\lambda_i$ differentiate and
the structural residual dominates. Also removed the claim that
float64 promotion "closes the gap" for Adaptive (the trained residual
is structural, not numerical).
